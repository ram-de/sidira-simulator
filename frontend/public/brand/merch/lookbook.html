Principle

The bench leaves the lab.

Goods follow the same law as the product. Every piece is either a record (ink on paper-natural goods) or a reading (signal lime on glass-dark goods). Printed traces are records of a labeled specimen run, never decoration: each carries its axis, its threshold, and its provenance. The aperture hexagon is the die-cut shape. Nothing is fully rounded, nothing has a gradient, and lime never touches a light garment.

Production notes

Pre-flight
Ink matching
Pantone values above are screen approximations. Proof against printed swatches before a run; lime must read fluorescent-adjacent on navy (389 C, or 802 C if the printer stocks it).
Text
All SVG text converts to outlines before handoff. Type is Archivo Narrow SemiBold, Archivo, and JetBrains Mono per DESIGN.md §5 (MVP stack is production).
Minimums
Mark reproduction floor is 8 mm (§1.5). Embroidery: satin stitch, thread ≥ 0.8 mm, mark no smaller than 50 mm.
Hairlines
Screen printing cannot hold an 8% opacity hairline. Axis furniture in the artwork is set at 25 to 55% paper; printer converts to halftone or nearest solid mix.
Garments
Heavyweight blanks only: 240 gsm tees, 400 gsm fleece. Natural / ecru for paper goods, blackout navy for glass goods. Vendor pick is decision D-03.
Refuse list
Transfers in full (§10): no gradients, no pills or circular die-cuts, no drop shadows or bevels on the mark, no rotation of the mark, no lime on light garments, no decorative traces.

Open decisions

Founder sign-off needed
  1. Is merch a brand-mode surface? STK-02 (duo hex) ships only if yes.

    DESIGN.md §1.3 sanctions duo for favicon, public site, press kit, social avatar, anniversary, and deck page one. Swag is public-facing like the press kit, so the working assumption is yes, for exactly one piece. If sanctioned, add merch to the §1.3 list in a DESIGN.md update.

  2. Approve the specimen numbers. 0.78 against threshold 0.70, 240 readings, 48 personas, 5 seeds, SPECIMEN-001.

    Honest-numbers law (§8.3) extended to print: the values are fictional but labeled as a specimen record, never presented as a live reading. Strictly, §8.4 binds traces to real run data and the label alone doesn't create an exception — so the decision is binary: either re-pin these to a real saved run, or add a one-line merch specimen exception to §8.4 in the same DESIGN.md update as D-01.

  3. Pick blanks and printer. Specs assume 240 gsm tees and 400 gsm fleece in natural and blackout navy.

    Pantone matching for ink and signal happens at proof stage with the chosen vendor.
