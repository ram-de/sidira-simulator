The Commute Loyalist p_streamly_01

n=769◇ UNCAL
tight · habitual rejects → adopts

Opens Streamly when they sit down on the train and listens until they reach the office: one forty-minute session, five mornings a week, almost always the same three playlists. They have had the app since 2023, never churned, never upgraded past the mid-tier plan, and treat a buffering gap on the underground as a defect. Reliability is the product to them; discovery is noise.

The Background Utility p_streamly_03

n=595◇ UNCAL
bimodal · ad-split rejects → adopts

Streamly is wallpaper. It runs for hours — focus playlists, ambient mixes, one radio station left on through the workday — and the listener rarely touches the controls once the sound is going. High total listening time, near-zero interaction. They are price sensitive, sit almost entirely on the ad-supported tier, and are the segment most exposed to, and most quietly worn down by, an aggressive ad load.
