◇ The Spread Stamp

Every reading wears its uncertainty on its sleeve.

Sidira's readings are uncalibrated estimates, and the instrument never pretends otherwise. An inferred figure carries its CI (±) and a calibration glyph — ◇ uncalibrated, ◆ graded. The hardest objection becomes a mark you can scan, not fine print to dismiss.

Candor is scoped to what the model inferred about people. Recorded facts — the cohort n, the seed, the model, the snapshot — print bare. An estimate that hides its spread is the bug; so is a fact wearing a fake one.
