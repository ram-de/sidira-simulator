**Specific Actionable Fixes**
- Across app: reduce Channel Legend/designator volume by 35–50%. Keep one legend per live reading panel, not every subpanel, subsection, rail, and footer.
- Across app: remove numbered section scaffolds (`01`, `02`, etc.) except true ordered flows. `crawl-setup`, `simulations-new`, `feature-pack`, and `agent-detail` read especially AI-scaffolded.
- Across app: enforce the signal budget. `simulation-results`, `agent-transcript`, and `app-shell` use signal for “positive,” “ship,” “up,” and emphasis. Signal should mean live reading only; positive/negative sentiment must render in ink with arm/provenance separate.
- `simulation-results.html`: fix bare readings in cohort table: `+0.71 / +0.40`, `+ positive`, agent counts, margin, and threshold comparisons need either `±`, CI, or clear classification as non-reading metadata.
- `personas-list.html`: persona share, trend deltas, and stability should share one compact Spread Stamp pattern. Right now only stability feels epistemically honest.
- `capability-detail.html`: choose Sheet for the route. If a live estimate belongs there, embed one restrained Console well inside the Sheet, not a dark masthead that inverts mid-route.
- `crawl-setup.html`: make it a high-efficiency configuration surface. Shrink/remove the Population Field, collapse capability URL groups, make the manifest the primary audit surface, and move failed-run history into a compact log row.
- `simulations-list.html`: demote the hero field to a narrow latest-reading strip or remove it. This page’s job is comparison, sorting, and resuming work.
- `agent-detail.html`: separate interrogation from analytics. Keep the isolated-particle chamber, but move cohort distribution and drivers into a collapsible evidence drawer.
- `simulation-results.html`: “SHIP CANDIDATE” is too final for “RAW · UNCALIBRATED · LEDGER EMPTY.” Use “PROMISING PRIOR” or “TEST FIRST” unless graded.
- Across Sheet pages: tighten prose. The em-dash-heavy, manifesto-like copy dilutes the instrument voice; records should read calmer and more archival.
- Across canvas effects: keep the Population Field only where it is the computation: results, new simulation manifest, persona build evaluation. On list/config/detail pages, use static posters or tiny isolated-particle motifs.
