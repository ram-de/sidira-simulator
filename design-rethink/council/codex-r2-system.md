1. **Verdict:** A serious, memorable system with a real point of view, but it is currently over-lawyered and over-theatrical; elite screens will require restraint, accessibility hardening, and fewer absolute gestures.

2. **CALIBRATED SCORE:** 82

3. **The 3 Strongest Things**
- **Console vs Sheet is a strong ontology.** Dark = live estimate, light = printed record is memorable, product-native, and teaches epistemology without copy.
- **Candor as visual law is excellent.** Spread Stamp, calibration glyphs, Channel Legend, and Provenance Spine turn uncertainty into interface grammar instead of disclaimer sludge.
- **The Population Field is directionally right.** Showing countable synthetic people becoming a distribution is the rare “signature effect” that can be substance, not decoration.

4. **Most Important Fixes To Reach Top-0.1%**
- **Reduce absolutes.** “Every score, timestamp, cohort n carries ±” is absurd in practice. Scope Spread Stamp to inferred/predicted outcomes only: score, lift, sentiment, confidence, weakness frequency, conversion estimate. Exempt identifiers, timestamps, seed, model, raw `n`.
- **Fix contrast claims.** `--sheet-signal #0FA882` on `--sheet #F3F4F7` is ~2.75:1, not AA. Use `oklch(0.43 0.12 168)` / approx `#00785F`. `--sheet-spread #857449` is ~4.16:1; darken to approx `#6F5E33`. `--text-faint #646E84` on `--console-700 #26314C` is ~2.5:1; reserve it for disabled only, never labels/tooltips.
- **Make uncalibrated drift finite and accessible.** “Never settles” becomes irritating and exclusionary. Define: uncalibrated drift max amplitude `2px`, opacity `0.45`, cycle `12–18s`, pauses after 20s idle, freezes on focus/hover/reduced motion. The glyph and copy carry the truth; motion should not punish attention.
- **Constrain the Population Field.** Clickable 1,200 particles is conceptually rich but operationally noisy. Use hover/click only above `24px` binned clusters; individual dots selectable after zoom or bin selection. Mobile gets static distribution + sampled agents list.
- **Tone down invented chrome.** Channel Legend on every live panel risks becoming decorative telemetry wallpaper. Require it only once per reading region; nested panels inherit provenance via a compact `CH1` reference.
- **Add component state matrices.** Buttons, inputs, tables, charts need explicit default/hover/focus/active/disabled/loading/error specs for both Console and Sheet. Top-tier systems are boringly complete at this layer.
- **Reconsider treatment red.** You say no valence color, then assign treatment `#F0796B`, which many users will read as danger. Shift treatment toward magenta/coral with less alarm: approx `oklch(0.72 0.10 345)`.

5. **Biggest “Trying Too Hard” Risk**
The system names too many signature ideas: Population Field, Spread Stamp, Channel Legend, Provenance Spine, Console↔Sheet Inversion, Living Manifest, Dim-Unlocked Trace. Individually, several are good. Together, they risk reading as a concept deck performing intelligence. Elite product design would keep the ontology, candor primitives, and one signature visualization, then make 80% of screens brutally quiet.
