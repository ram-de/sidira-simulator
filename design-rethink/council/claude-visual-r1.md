# Claude visual verdict — Round 1 (by eye, rendered PNGs @1440px)

Reviewed renders: landing, simulation-results, persona-detail (Sheet), personas-new, app-shell.

## Verdict: STRONG direction, NOT yet top-0.1%. ≈78/100 normalized. "Close."

### What's working (keep)
- **The Console/Sheet dual-ontology is the win.** Dark = live estimate, light = printed record. Original, defensible, perfectly matched to a measurement/simulation product. It reads as intelligence.
- **Data-viz as hero** = thesis delivered. Landing's distribution-curve hero, the dashboard's +6.2pp reading + chart, the wizard's particle "cohort settle," the big confident ±-bearing number readings — all excellent and on-brand.
- **Type & hierarchy** strong; the big-number readings are confident; Sheet serif + left provenance spine is genuinely editorial.
- Landing ≈ Linear/Vercel grade. Dashboard ≈ premium quant terminal.

### What blocks top-0.1% (fix in R2)
1. **Density / breathing room.** simulation-results and app-shell are *packed* — top-0.1% pages breathe more. Add air, reduce simultaneous panels per viewport, let the hero reading dominate.
2. **Effects too subtle as stills** given "no holds barred." Backgrounds are quiet matrices; charts are flat. Push: more depth/glow/dimensional WebGL on the heroes, a real signature showpiece per Console page — while keeping the "computation not decoration" taste rule. Make a designer stop scrolling.
3. **Honor the system's own laws** (jury was right):
   - **Candor law:** ~30 bare numbers print without spread on Sheet record pages. Every reading must wear ±ci (or be marked exact).
   - **AA contrast:** Sheet signal-green (#0FA882) fails 4.5:1 as text on light cards — darken to ~#0A7D60 for text.
   - **Signal budget:** hero regions stack 3–4 accent carriers; demote to one.
   - **Uncalibrated drift:** uncalibrated readings are specified to drift and never settle, but the wizard/field render them settled — a self-contradiction in the marquee honesty mechanic.
   - **No valence color:** persona-detail colors ▲higher green — direction must be plain ink, glyph carries it.
4. **Scoring scale was inconsistent** (some jurors 0–10, some 0–100) — enforce 0–100 calibrated in R2.

### Net
Direction is keepable and genuinely distinctive. R2 = push effects harder (tastefully), add breathing room, enforce the candor/contrast/signal/drift laws, consistent scoring. Then re-judge.
