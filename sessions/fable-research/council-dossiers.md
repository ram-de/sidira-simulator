## [causal-transport] The Transport Ladder: a partial-identification data-fusion engine that moves measured effects INTO the customer instead of demanding the customer's data

**One-liner:** Stop asking the customer for the data to identify the effect; identify the effect in source domains you control (Senu factory, pooled customer exhaust, public RCTs), then transport it into the target customer via Bareinboim-Pearl transport formulas whose only target-side input is exactly what customers already give you — persona aggregates and segment tables — and ship Manski-style sign bounds that tighten monotonically as the customer climbs the data ladder.

### Mechanism
The reframe: the surviving g-formula architecture needs tier-3 data because it fits the response surface E[Y | mediator, X] inside the target customer. Transportability theory (Pearl/Bareinboim) says you don't have to: if the response surface is measured in a SOURCE environment, the target-population effect is the transport formula ATE* = Σ_z P*(z) · E_source[Y | do(change), z] — and the only target-side object it needs is P*(z), the covariate/segment distribution. That is literally what a persona pack IS. The expensive object (the causal surface) moves to Sidira's side; the cheap object (the mix) stays the customer's only ask.

Pipeline, end to end: (1) LIBRARY. Build a mechanism-tagged effect library: every Senu triple, every public RCT (Cookie Cats, Hillstrom, portfolio cases), and — with consent — every customer's A/B exhaust, each tagged by the already-validated LLM-translator with a mechanism spec (taxonomy pattern: cannibalization/trade-down/gross-vs-net/…; mediator; sibling metric; negative controls) and stored as CONDITIONAL effect surfaces E_s[Y|do(x), z] per segment z, not scalar ATEs. Fit a hierarchical Bayesian model over (pattern × context) — the empirical prior over experiment effects that Azevedo et al. showed is near-zero-centered with fat tails, which structurally kills the always-predicts-lift failure. (2) TRANSLATE. A new hypothesis → mechanism spec (existing translator role, unchanged). Match against library patterns; construct the selection diagram: which effect-modifiers differ between source and target (S-nodes). (3) TRANSPORT. Reweight each matched source surface by the target's P*(z) from persona aggregates/segment tables. Where multiple source environments exist, compute the effect under each and INTERSECT the resulting bounds (the meta-learners-across-environments construction, arXiv:2406.02464). Where an S-node sits on an unmeasured modifier, don't point-identify — emit Manski-style partial-identification bounds on the sign. (4) VERDICT. Bounds exclude zero → confident direction (CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS the PM's bet, with the source receipts). Bounds straddle zero → honest "test it." This makes abstention a computed interval width, not an LLM mood. (5) BRIDGE. For long-run business metrics, learn the surrogate index E[Y_long | S_short] (Athey–Chetty–Imbens–Kang) on the library side; the customer only ever reports short-run cheap signals. (6) GUARD. At tiers 2–3, replace the failed single negative-control guard with the proximal/double-negative-control confounding bridge (Miao–Tchetgen, arXiv:1808.04945): the translator nominates a treatment-proxy AND outcome-proxy pair; the bridge function corrects unmeasured confounding the single NC merely detected. (7) CALIBRATE. Tier-2 exhaust is used not as training data but as a per-customer backtest: score transported predictions against their own past measured outcomes → a customer-specific transport-error certificate and empirical-Bayes shrinkage of the library prior toward their scale (the Meta replication corpus shows exactly this pooling beats face-value estimates, MAE 1.012 vs 1.280). The Senu→customer external-validity gap is itself modeled as a transport edge and measured, never assumed away.

### Data ladder
- **tier0_persona_only:** Persona aggregates = P*(z) transport weights (their correct mathematical role — never LLM seeds). Deliverable: pattern-matched directional call with intersection bounds over library environments; abstain when no pattern matches or bounds straddle zero. Expected coverage tracks the 80/20 taxonomy bet: answers on the recognizable ~80%, honest silence on the tail. Directional accuracy on the answered subset should approach source-measured accuracy (target ≥0.7 to ship; the Meta corpus shows cross-experiment pooling beats per-case estimates even for exact replications, so pooled library-priors weighted by target mix are not a hopeful guess). Day-1 value: ordinal triage of a 40-hypothesis backlog + first CONTRADICTS catches on library-covered patterns.
- **tier1_aggregates:** Segment tables/moments give a finer P*(z): segment-level reweighting, per-segment direction ('wins overall, loses in casual'), and materially tighter intersection bounds because more S-nodes become measured. Plus the surrogate bridge: customer reports short-run aggregate movements of cheap signals; the library-side surrogate index maps them to the long-run business metric (Athey et al.: 6 quarters of proxies recovered 9-year impacts). This tier is the sweet spot: near-tier-3 directional trust on library-covered mechanisms with zero per-user data shared.
- **tier2_ab_exhaust:** Past A/B results = free labels for a per-customer transport-error certificate (sealed backtest: 'transported prediction matched k of your n past experiments'), empirical-Bayes calibration of effect scale, local validation of the Prentice surrogacy assumption, and library enrichment. This is where trust becomes quantified per customer rather than argued — and the certificate itself is a sellable artifact.
- **tier3_event_level:** The existing surviving architecture, upgraded: full in-customer g-formula mediation sim (AMBDOM-proven, 12/12 on magnitude flips) with the proximal double-NC bridge replacing the failed single-NC guard for naturally confounded exhaust. Bounds collapse toward point-identified direction; transport is only needed for mechanisms absent from their own data. Tier 3 becomes the premium accuracy rung, not the entry ticket.

### Why it survives the negative results
(1) Persona role-play 0.29: personas never generate an outcome here — they enter as the covariate distribution P*(z) inside a transport formula whose effect surfaces are MEASURED in real experiments. Remove the persona pack and you lose reweighting precision, not the verdict source; remove the source experiments and the system abstains rather than guesses. Mechanically the opposite information flow: numbers→persona-weighted aggregate, not persona-prompt→LLM-imagined number. (2) Naive LLM ~0.50: no LLM prediction exists anywhere in the path; the LLM does mechanism tagging and S-node nomination — the translator role that already survived. A mis-tag degrades matching (recoverable, auditable), never fabricates a direction. (3) Council-of-priors: no model votes. Where the old design used cross-model disagreement as a caution dial, this uses cross-ENVIRONMENT disagreement — intersection-bound width across source datasets — which is data heterogeneity, not shared LLM prior. Agreement of environments tightens bounds because the environments are statistically independent; agreement of LLMs was the confidently-wrong mode because their priors are correlated. (4) Magnitude-blindness / words-only ceiling: the magnitude information enters numerically — source effect surfaces × target segment mix — precisely the channel AMBDOM proved works (grounded 1.00 vs LLM 0.00). This proposal generalizes the winning mechanism (compute the net from numbers) to source-measured surfaces so the numbers no longer have to live in the target customer. (5) Production always-predicts-lift: the library prior is empirically centered near zero with fat tails (Bing corpus), and Senu deliberately over-samples negatives; the system's default output is 'no detectable effect / test it,' and a lift claim requires bounds excluding zero. Optimism is structurally impossible, not prompted away. (6) Fragile-Families ceiling: the estimand is segment-level, never individual; partial identification encodes the ceiling as interval width. Where behavior is unpredictable, bounds straddle zero and the product says so — the ceiling becomes the abstain rule rather than a hidden error term.

### Testable experiment
Sealed leave-one-environment-out transport ladder, runnable on assets already in the repo (research/engine-strength-campaign runstore discipline; ~1–2 weeks, zero customer ask). SETUP: source library = AMBDOM/GNCE synthetic battery cells + Hillstrom + portfolio cases, each mechanism-tagged by the existing translator. Held-out target = Cookie Cats (real, counter-intuitive negative, per-user data available for truth). LADDER SIMULATION: from Cookie Cats derive ONLY tier-0/1 artifacts (segment marginals / moment tables, then seal away the per-user rows). Pre-register, fingerprint-sealed before scoring: (a) transported sign + intersection bounds at tier 0, (b) at tier 1, (c) surrogate-bridge prediction of day-7 retention from day-1 signals learned entirely on source data. Score against measured truth, vs three baselines: coin flip, best frontier LLM one-shot (known ~0.50), and the full tier-3 in-customer g-formula (known upper anchor, 1.00 on flips). Repeat leave-one-out over every battery cell as target for n. KILL CRITERIA (pre-registered): kill if tier-1 bounds either (i) exclude zero on <30% of pattern-matched cases (vacuous — no value below tier 3), or (ii) exclude zero with the WRONG sign at >chance (transport is actively misleading). PROMOTE if tier-1 directional accuracy on the answered subset ≥0.7 with honest abstention on the rest. Week 2 extension: proximal double-NC on the naturally-confounded benchmark that broke the single-NC guard — does the bridge recover the sign the guard could only flag?

### Failure modes
(1) The 80/20 taxonomy is false: real hypotheses don't match library patterns, so tier-0/1 answers almost nothing — the whole down-ladder value rides on pattern recurrence; the leave-one-out experiment measures match rate directly. (2) S-nodes on the mechanism itself: if the effect-modifiers that differ between Senu/source populations and the customer are unmeasured in persona aggregates, bounds go vacuous (correct behavior, but commercially it's 'abstain-ware'). (3) Surrogate paradox: Prentice violations can flip the sign of the bridged long-run effect; must run Athey et al.'s bias diagnostics and validate surrogacy per customer at tier 2 before trusting the bridge at tier 1. (4) Library scale: ~6 public RCTs + early Senu output is a thin prior; early tier-0 answers will be dominated by Senu, whose respondents ≠ customer users — mitigated by modeling Senu→customer as an explicit measured transport edge, but if that edge is weak the factory's value is delayed, not free. (5) LLM mis-tagging of mechanism specs silently matches the wrong library pattern — needs a tag-audit loop (route low-confidence tags to human review, Bridgewater-style). (6) Winner's curse in ingested customer exhaust biases the library optimistic unless shrinkage-corrected at ingestion. (7) Consent/pooling friction: cross-customer exhaust pooling needs contractual cover; the design partner confidentiality episode shows this is a real constraint — the library must work (degraded) on Senu+public alone. (8) Bounds are honest but unfamiliar: buyers may read intervals as weakness; the product surface must translate bound-excludes-zero into the existing CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS/TEST-IT vocabulary, never raw intervals.

### Buildability
Already exists: causal_estimator.py + negative-control guard (research-grade); the LLM mechanism-spec translator role (validated in GNCE); the g-formula mediation sim (AMBDOM harness); the AMBDOM/GNCE synthetic factory + sealed runstore/LEDGER discipline for pre-registration; Senu export plumbing end-to-end (#547–549) with role-named arms — Senu triples land in exactly the (population, change, outcome) shape the library needs; persona packs + segment tables (the tier-0/1 substrate) are already the product's easy onboarding artifact; portfolio-benchmark/PORTFOLIO_CASES.json as public-RCT seeds. To build: (a) effect-library schema + mechanism tagging pass over existing cases — days; (b) transport/reweighting module (weighted g-computation over library surfaces given P*(z)) — ~1 week, plain Python on the existing estimator; (c) bounds-intersection layer per arXiv:2406.02464 — ~1 week; (d) surrogate-index model (standard supervised fit on library data) — days; (e) proximal/double-NC estimator to replace the single-NC guard — the hardest research item, ~2–3 weeks including validation on the benchmark that broke the guard. The 2-week kill experiment needs only (a)–(c) at prototype grade. Product-grade wiring into the watchdog flow (§5e) is the same rewiring already on the roadmap — this proposal changes what Step 2 falls back to (transported bounds instead of LLM narrative + council), which is a strict simplification of the runtime.

### Literature
- Bareinboim & Pearl (2016), "Causal inference and the data-fusion problem," PNAS 113(27) — https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1510507113; and Pearl & Bareinboim (2014), "External Validity: From do-calculus to Transportability Across Populations," Statistical Science — https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r404-reprint.pdf — The core engine: complete algorithms deciding when an effect measured in source environments is transportable to a target, and the transport formula ATE* = Σ_z P*(z)·E_source[Y|do(x),z] whose only target-side requirement is the covariate distribution — i.e., the persona pack. Selection diagrams give the audit trail for WHY a case is or isn't transportable.
- Athey, Chetty, Imbens & Kang, "The Surrogate Index," NBER w26463 / Review of Economic Studies 2025 — https://www.nber.org/papers/w26463 — Moves the expensive short-run→long-run outcome mapping to Sidira's library side; customer supplies only cheap short-run aggregate signals. Their validation (6 quarters of proxies recover 9-year effects) plus their bias characterization gives the exact test for when the bridge is trustworthy.
- Miao, Geng & Tchetgen Tchetgen, "A confounding bridge approach for double negative control inference on causal effects" — https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.04945; proximal causal inference framework (Tchetgen Tchetgen et al.) — The named fix for the one failed component: the single negative-control guard detects confounding but can't correct it and failed a hard naturally-confounded benchmark. Double-NC/proximal uses a treatment-proxy + outcome-proxy pair and a bridge function to identify effects UNDER unmeasured confounding — turning tier-2 exhaust from untrustworthy to usable.
- Manski, partial identification / no-assumptions bounds (Manski 1990; Tamer 2010 review — https://scholar.harvard.edu/files/tamer/files/pie.pdf) — The honesty layer: at low tiers, unverifiable transport assumptions yield bounds, not points. Manski's bottom-up discipline (worst-case bounds, then add assumptions transparently) is exactly the sign+confidence/abstain product contract, made rigorous.
- Azevedo, Deng, Montiel Olea, Rao & Weyl, "A/B Testing with Fat Tails," Journal of Political Economy 128(12), 2020 — https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/710607 — Measured the empirical prior over real experiment effects (Bing corpus): mostly ~null, fat tails. Justifies the library's zero-centered hierarchical prior — the structural cure for always-predicts-lift — and grounds the skip/test decision economics.
- "Meta-Learners for Partially-Identified Treatment Effects Across Multiple Environments" — https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.02464 — Directly the tier-0/1 estimator: learns bounds on effects when identification fails locally, and intersects bounds across multiple source environments — the mechanism that replaces model-council 'agreement' with statistically independent environment agreement.
- "Breaking the Winner's Curse with Bayesian Hybrid Shrinkage" — https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12867 (Meta replication corpus, 167 paired experiments) — Empirical proof on a real experimentation platform that cross-experiment pooling predicts replication outcomes better than each experiment's own face-value estimate (MAE 1.012 vs 1.280) — the quantitative case that a pooled library transported into a target beats naive per-case reads, and the winner's-curse correction needed when ingesting customer exhaust.
- Degtiar & Rose, "A Review of Generalizability and Transportability" — https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.11904 — Practical estimator menu (weighting, outcome-model, doubly-robust transport estimators) and diagnostics for the engineer building the transport module; maps assumptions to what is checkable at each data tier.

### RED TEAM — verdict: **REVISE**
Fatal flaws:
- The Bareinboim–Pearl guarantee does not cover this setting. Transportability moves the effect of the SAME intervention under SHARED structural equations across populations that differ only in S-node distributions. Sidira's sources (Senu phone micro-games, an email RCT, a mobile-game gate) and targets (arbitrary customer products) differ in intervention, outcome definition, AND causal structure. The 'mechanism tag' match that bridges them is an LLM verbal-analogy judgment — so the engine's actual identification story collapses to reference-class analogy plus reweighting, and reference-class analogy already measured NULL in the campaign. The do-calculus is doing branding work, not identification work.
- Magnitude-blindness re-enters at the matching step. AMBDOM's core finding is that identical verbal descriptions map to opposite signs depending on magnitudes. Therefore two library cases with the same mechanism tag can legitimately carry opposite-signed surfaces; the pattern-conditional prior then straddles zero and tier-0/1 abstains on exactly the magnitude-flip slice that is the wedge. The information that decides a flip is the TARGET's mediator/feature magnitudes — tier-3 data — not the segment mix P*(z). Tier-0/1 would not have scored 1.00 on AMBDOM; it would have (honestly) said 'test it' 12/12 times. Abstain-ware is the center of the distribution, not failure-mode #2's tail case.
- The pre-registered kill experiment is rigged toward a false PROMOTE. Leave-one-out over AMBDOM/GNCE battery cells transports between environments drawn from the same synthetic generator — transportability holds by construction. Passing it says nothing about cross-product transport, which has exactly two real test edges available (Hillstrom↔Cookie Cats), i.e., n≈2 for the claim the product actually needs.
- Intersection-of-bounds reproduces the council's confidently-wrong mode with data. Intersection is valid only if EVERY source is a valid transport source; the proposal's justification ('environments are statistically independent') is asserted and false for the realistic library — all Senu cells share one respondent pool, incentive scheme, and minutes-scale horizon, and pooled customer exhaust shares winner's-curse selection. Correlated-bias environments agreeing tightens bounds around a wrong sign — the data-flavored 0/12 agreement failure. Worse, the cited tier-0/1 estimator (Schweisthal et al., arXiv:2406.02464 — real paper) is misapplied: it does partial identification of CATE treating the ENVIRONMENT as an instrument within observed multi-environment data; it is not a transport-into-unseen-target-given-only-P*(z) method.
- The surrogate bridge has no data source. Athey–Chetty–Imbens–Kang requires library-side linkage of short-run surrogates to genuinely long-run outcomes in a comparable population. Senu produces minutes-scale micro-task outcomes; the public corpus tops out at day-7/two-week metrics. Nothing in Sidira's reachable library can fit E[Y_long|S_short], yet tier-1's 'sweet spot' claim leans on it.
- Library bootstrap is circular and thin. Today the library is ~6 public RCTs plus synthetic cells; Senu is undeployed and founder-gated with its own month-4–5 viability gate, and the campaign's own B2 finding was that 10 realistic public datasets are all one-sign — the real flip cases the library most needs do not exist in acquirable data. The Senu→customer transport edge can only be MEASURED against a customer's tier-2/3 data, so tier-0 trust presupposes the very data ask the proposal claims to eliminate. Also, segment-conditional surfaces E_s[Y|do(x),z] from ₹40/respondent studies (n in the hundreds) are noise per segment; hierarchical shrinkage to the near-zero Azevedo prior drives the default answer to 'test it' everywhere.
- Diligence-grade cite sloppiness: the winner's-curse paper is real (Meta, 167 paired replications, MAE 1.012 vs 1.280) but lives at arXiv:2511.06318; and the proposal's strongest empirical crutch (that corpus) is about re-estimating THE SAME experiment under selection, not about transporting effects across products — supportive of tier-2 shrinkage, silent on tier-0 transport.

Evidence collisions: Checked against all six negative results. (#1 persona role-play 0.29: NO collision — P*(z) reweighting is genuinely the opposite information flow (measured numbers → persona-weighted aggregate); the defense holds and is the proposal's best idea. (#2 reference-class analogy NULL: DIRECT collision at the matching step. The defense ('we transport measured surfaces, not LLM recall') only works if verbal mechanism-similarity predicts effect-surface similarity — the measured NULL is evidence against precisely that exchangeability assumption. Defense fails as stated. (#3 council-of-priors: collision in disguise. Cross-environment intersection inherits the agreement-is-confidently-wrong mode whenever sources share bias, which a Senu-dominated library guarantees (one pool, one incentive structure, one horizon). The 'environments are independent' defense is asserted, not established; treat-Senu-as-one-environment is the missing fix. (#4 magnitude-blindness/AMBDOM: defense holds at tier 3 (it IS the surviving g-formula) but fails at tier 0/1 — flip-deciding magnitudes are target-side per-user quantities absent from P*(z); the system would have abstained on, not solved, the AMBDOM flips, so the wedge does not move down the ladder as claimed. (#5 production blind tests: would not have been wrong, but would have been mute — Case 5's lesson is that the edge is gross-vs-net unit economics + estimability, which needs customer-specific margin/refund numbers no library transports and no persona aggregate contains; tier-0/1 emits vacuous bounds exactly where the sellable catches were. (#6 Fragile-Families ceiling: no collision — encoding the ceiling as interval width/abstention is correct and is the proposal's second-best idea.

Data-ask honesty: Partially honest, substantially displaced. The genuine reduction: IF transport held, the response surface moves to Sidira's side and the target-side ask really is just a covariate mix — a correct and novel reading of what persona packs are mathematically for. But four displacements hide in it: (1) onto Senu — the library is the new data ask, it is undeployed, expensive, founder-gated, and its counter-intuitive-case coverage is the whole bet; (2) onto cross-customer exhaust pooling, which the design-partner confidentiality scrub demonstrated is contractually hard, so the degraded Senu+public library is the realistic one; (3) onto the customer anyway at trust time — the per-customer transport-error certificate (the only quantified-trust artifact) requires tier-2 exhaust, so confident stamps still cost data; tier-0 alone yields ordinal triage, not CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS; (4) onto P*(z) itself — the S-admissible modifier set is a JOINT distribution over behavioral effect-modifiers (usage intensity, mediator baselines), not the marketing-persona marginals customers hand over easily; an adequate P*(z) quietly drifts back up the ladder. Also note the awkward product fact: Sidira's existing persona packs are themselves built from customer feature stores by sidira-persona-worker, so for current customers 'tier-0 only' is partly the tier-3 data wearing a summary hat.

**Strongest version (repair):** Keep three things unconditionally: (a) personas recast as P*(z) transport weights, never LLM seeds — the correct mathematical role and the cleanest survival of the 0.29 result; (b) abstention as computed interval width, not LLM mood; (c) the zero-centered fat-tail library prior as the structural kill of always-predicts-lift. Then repair: (1) INVERT THE LADDER COMMERCIALLY — make tier 2 (past A/B exhaust, a spreadsheet of experiment metadata + per-segment reads) the entry artifact instead of tier 3. It is nearly as cheap for the customer as a persona pack, has real identification content, and is where the one solid empirical crutch (Meta winner's-curse corpus, arXiv:2511.06318) actually applies. The wedge pitch becomes: 'send us your last 30 experiments; we show you which k we'd have called correctly, sealed before scoring' — the transport-error certificate as the FIRST deliverable, not a later rung. (2) Demote tier 0/1 to ordinal backlog triage and CONTRADICTS-screening only — no confident stamps ever issue from transported bounds alone. (3) Replace intersection-of-bounds with random-effects hierarchical pooling reporting between-source heterogeneity τ²; treat all of Senu as ONE environment; allow bound-tightening only across provably independent source families. (4) Fix the experiment: leave-one-REAL-environment-out only (Hillstrom↔Cookie Cats↔portfolio cases), synthetic LOO demoted to a harness negative control; add a pre-registered 'same-tag, opposite-sign' audit of the library that directly measures how often mechanism tags are sign-ambiguous — this is the cheapest possible test of whether the matching step re-imports magnitude-blindness, and it can run this week on existing cases with zero customer ask. (5) Drop the surrogate bridge until a library source with genuinely long-run outcomes exists; keep proximal double-NC exactly where it already is — a tier-3 research track, not a named fix. (6) Pre-register match-rate economics on a real backlog: tier-1 ships only if it answers ≥30% of a real customer's hypothesis list at ≥0.7 directional accuracy on the answered subset, with the abstain-rate reported as a headline number, not a footnote.

## [outcome-grounded-training] B-PFN: A Behavioral Prior-Fitted Network — amortize the g-formula once on expensive data, condition on cheap customer aggregates at inference

**One-liner:** Train a numeric (non-LLM) transformer once, TabPFN-style, on millions of simulated experiments from the failure-mode taxonomy plus Senu triples and cross-customer A/B exhaust, so that per-customer prediction is pure in-context Bayesian inference on whatever data tier the customer has — with an antisymmetric arm-swap head that makes an optimism/pessimism prior architecturally impossible, and a posterior that widens honestly as the conditioning data gets cheaper.

### Mechanism
The core move is amortized inference (the Bridgewater lesson applied structurally): spend the expensive data once at TRAINING time, so INFERENCE needs only cheap data. Prior-fitted networks (Müller et al. ICLR 2022; TabPFN, Hollmann et al. Nature 2025) prove the recipe: a transformer trained on millions of synthetic data-generating processes performs posterior predictive inference in-context on a new small dataset, no fine-tuning. CausalPFN (arXiv:2506.07918) and Do-PFN (arXiv:2506.06039, NeurIPS 2025 spotlight) extend this to treatment effects: trained on simulated SCMs with interventions, they estimate CATEs on new observational data out of the box, beating per-dataset estimators on IHDP/ACIC/Lalonde. Nobody has built the product-experiment version. We have the unique assets to: the AMBDOM/GNCE generators ARE a structural prior over the counter-intuitive taxonomy, and Senu manufactures real triples.

Pretraining corpus (the moat): (1) millions of synthetic experiment-DGPs sampled from an SCM prior parameterized by the failure-mode taxonomy — cannibalization, trade-down, substitution, novelty-decay, gross-vs-net, mix-flip — extending the existing AMBDOM/GNCE generators; (2) Senu (population → change → measured outcome) triples, over-sampled on the taxonomy; (3) cross-customer A/B exhaust; (4) public uplift corpora (Criteo 25M, Hillstrom; Cookie Cats sealed held-out). Each training episode = (context C, mechanism spec m, arm pair (A,B), true effect distribution).

Objective: predict the posterior over the signed effect on each metric (headline + guardrails + per-segment), conditioned on C and m. Two non-negotiable tricks: (a) TIER-DROPOUT — during training, randomly ablate C down the ladder (full rows → A/B exhaust episodes → segment/moment tables → persona aggregates only), so ONE model serves every tier and its posterior widens honestly as context shrinks; abstain = wide posterior, calibrated by conformal risk control on sealed real experiments. (b) ANTISYMMETRIC HEAD — output s(A,B) = g(C,m,A,B) − g(C,m,B,A) plus arm-swap augmentation, so the marginal directional prediction is exactly symmetric by construction (RankNet-style); a llama-optimism or Opus-pessimism prior is not down-weighted, it is unrepresentable. Counter-intuitive oversampling then shapes likelihood SENSITIVITY, not the prior.

Runtime: the frontier LLM stays translator/narrator only — hypothesis + crawl → mechanism-spec tokens m (mediator, sibling, negative control, confounders) that become conditioning input; it never emits a verdict. The B-PFN computes direction + confidence; the negative-control watchdog and conformal gate stamp CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS/CANT-VALIDATE. At tier 3, the per-customer g-formula (GBM response surfaces — Grinsztajn et al. 2022: trees still win on medium tabular data) remains the authoritative estimator; the B-PFN is its transferable prior and cross-check. Bonus: the posterior supports expected-value-of-information — tell the customer exactly which ONE aggregate table would shrink uncertainty most, converting the onboarding ask from "give us your feature store" to a targeted incremental ladder climb.

### Data ladder
- **tier0_persona_only:** Persona aggregates become conditioning tokens (segment weights, usage moments) — crucially these carry NUMBERS words don't. Deliverable: honest triage of a hypothesis backlog (posterior-ordered skip/test ranking), wide-posterior abstain on most verdicts, plus an EVOI report ('send us the revenue-by-segment table and cases 3, 7, 12 become callable'). Expected confident-subset directional accuracy ~0.60 at ~20–30% coverage — sold as prioritization + a data-ask roadmap, never as verdicts.
- **tier1_aggregates:** Segment tables / moments resolve the COMPOSITION-DRIVEN half of the taxonomy (mix-flip, gross-vs-net, trade-down): these flips are arithmetic over aggregate ratios (Simpson-type), identifiable without per-user rows. Target: near-g-formula accuracy on that sub-slice (pre-registered win = ≥10/12 on composition-driven AMBDOM flip cells from aggregates alone, vs LLM 0.00), abstain on mediator-driven flips. ~0.75–0.85 on the confident subset at ~40–50% coverage. This is the tier that moves real value down the ladder.
- **tier2_ab_exhaust:** Past A/B episodes enter in-context as few-shot experiments: the model learns THIS customer's effect-size scale and responsiveness (empirical-Bayes shrinkage à la Azevedo et al. JPE 2020 — Bing's fat-tailed effect distribution, top 2% of ideas = 74.8% of gains), and the exhaust doubles as per-customer conformal calibration labels. Unlocks the CONTRADICTS product on past wins (external-validity audit with receipts). ~60–70% coverage; confidence now customer-calibrated, not global.
- **tier3_event_level:** Full feature store: the per-customer g-formula + negative-control watchdog remain authoritative (the measured 1.00 on magnitude-flips regime); B-PFN serves as prior, amortized fast-path, and disagreement flag (B-PFN vs g-formula divergence = investigate). Ceiling unchanged — tier 3 is now the premium rung, not the entry ticket.

### Why it survives the negative results
(1) Persona role-play 0.29: personas here are never identities an LLM inhabits — they are numeric conditioning statistics fed to a from-scratch tabular transformer trained against MEASURED outcomes (verifiable reward). No role-play exists anywhere in the verdict path. (2) Naive LLM ~0.50 / magnitude-blindness: the disproof says the information is not in the WORDS — correct, and this method never asks words to carry it. Magnitudes enter as numbers in the context set; where the needed magnitude (a mediator response curve) is absent from the tier's aggregates, tier-dropout training makes the posterior wide → abstain, rather than a confident guess. The tier-1 claim is falsifiable precisely because composition-driven flips ARE arithmetic over aggregates. (3) Council-of-priors made it worse: no ensembling of LLM opinions occurs; one model, one calibrated posterior. The only 'panel' logic retained is the validated abstain-siren polarity — disagreement (B-PFN vs g-formula, or watchdog distrust) raises caution; agreement never substitutes for data. (4) Model strength doesn't fix it: irrelevant by construction — the verdict model is not a pretrained text LLM, and the antisymmetric head makes ANY constant directional prior (optimism or pessimism) mathematically unrepresentable: E[s] under arm-swap symmetry is 0 unless the context data breaks the tie. This is a provable kill, not a mitigation. (5) Production engine tracks intuition: the deployed failure is the LLM-composer-as-predictor; here the LLM is demoted to translator (same as the surviving architecture), the watchdog stays as gate, and training oversamples exactly the counter-intuitive taxonomy the production engine gets wrong. (6) Fragile-Families ceiling: the model predicts AGGREGATE effect posteriors, never individuals; per-person outputs remain labeled calibrated fiction; the conformal gate + wide-posterior abstain encode 'unpredictable in principle' as an explicit output class instead of pretending coverage.

### Testable experiment
TIER-LADDER-1 (sealed, pre-registered, scratchpad-only, ~10 days, one GPU or CPU-scale). Build: extend the existing AMBDOM/GNCE generators into a DGP prior sampler over the 6-failure-mode taxonomy; train a nano-scale B-PFN (~10–30M params, nanoTabPFN codebase) with tier-dropout and the antisymmetric head; no Senu spend, no customer data. Pre-register four cells before any scoring: (A) the 12 AMBDOM magnitude-flip cells with context RESTRICTED to tier-1 segment aggregates (per-user rows withheld) — win = ≥10/12 direction on composition-driven flips (vs LLM 0.00, chance 0.50, tier-3 g-formula 1.00); (B) real Cookie Cats collapsed to a segment table — must recover the counter-intuitive negative sign from aggregates alone; (C) ablation — remove the antisymmetric head, measure the induced marginal direction prior (predict it reappears; with the head, arm-swap flip rate must be exactly 100%); (D) negative control — shuffled/permuted aggregates must produce wide-posterior abstain, not confident calls. Kill criterion: if (A) fails, the move-value-down-the-ladder thesis is dead and the feature-store ask is genuinely irreducible — worth knowing for ₹0 before the ₹20-lakh Senu phase-1 gate; if (A) passes but (B) fails, it's a sim2real prior-mismatch problem and Senu data is the fix, which re-prioritizes the factory.

### Failure modes
(1) Prior mismatch / sim2real: PFNs are Bayesian under THEIR prior — off-prior they can be confidently wrong; mitigations are the negative-control watchdog staying as gate, a prior-likelihood OOD flag routing to abstain, and Senu real triples progressively replacing synthetic mass (this is the factory's real job). (2) The ladder has a floor: mediator-DRIVEN flips (novel mechanism whose response curve lives only in per-user event data) are not identifiable from aggregates at any model quality — tier 1 buys the composition-driven half of the taxonomy, and the proposal must say so or it becomes the crystal ball again. (3) Senu external validity: Indian panel respondents ≠ customer users; transfer must be validated on held-out real RCTs per the existing discipline, never assumed. (4) Conformal calibration needs dozens of real labeled experiments per stratum — early customers get global (weaker) guarantees until tier-2 exhaust accrues. (5) Taxonomy leakage: train and eval DGPs from the same generator family inflates results — the sealed real cases (Cookie Cats, Hillstrom) are the only honest yardstick, and n is tiny. (6) Tier-0 coverage may be too thin to anchor a paid product alone — tier 0 must be sold as triage + EVOI data-roadmap, with the wedge landing at tiers 1–2. (7) At tier 3 the B-PFN may add nothing over the plain g-formula (CausalPFN's edge shrinks with data size) — acceptable, since its entire purpose is tiers 0–2.

### Buildability
Already in the stack: the AMBDOM/GNCE synthetic experiment generators (the seed of the DGP prior — this is the single biggest head start), causal_estimator.py with the 3-part negative-control guard, the sealed append-only runstore + LEDGER discipline for pre-registration, persona-pack and feature-store pipelines (the tier-0/tier-3 data objects exist in production), the Senu export → (current-state, change, outcome) plumbing (tickets #547–#549, runs end-to-end), and the LLM mechanism-spec translator pattern from GNCE. Off-the-shelf: TabPFN/nanoTabPFN and CausalPFN/Do-PFN open-source codebases and checkpoints (baseline before training anything). Must build: (a) the taxonomy-parameterized DGP prior sampler generalizing AMBDOM (~1 week), (b) PFN training loop with tier-dropout context encoder + antisymmetric head (~1 week on nanoTabPFN), (c) conformal gating wrapper (~days), (d) later: Senu-triple ingestion into the training corpus and per-customer exhaust episodes. Effort: TIER-LADDER-1 feasibility spike ≤2 weeks, one engineer, single GPU; production-candidate model + watchdog integration ~2–3 months; the long pole is the Senu corpus, which is already the planned ₹20-lakh phase-1 with its month-4–5 learning-curve gate — this proposal changes what that data trains (an amortized in-context model, not a per-case fine-tune), not whether to collect it.

### Literature
- Hollmann et al., 'Accurate predictions on small data with a tabular foundation model' (TabPFN v2), Nature, Jan 2025 — Existence proof for the whole bet: a transformer pretrained on synthetic DGPs does in-context Bayesian prediction on small real tabular datasets, beating GBMs up to ~10k rows with no per-task training — exactly the 'train once on expensive data, infer on cheap data' amortization Sidira needs.
- Müller et al., 'Transformers Can Do Bayesian Inference' (Prior-Data Fitted Networks), ICLR 2022 — The theoretical foundation: PFN training minimizes KL to the true posterior predictive under the sampled prior — so tier-dropout training provably yields tier-conditional posteriors, i.e., honest uncertainty that widens as customer data shrinks.
- Balazadeh et al., 'CausalPFN: Amortized Causal Effect Estimation via In-Context Learning', arXiv:2506.07918 (Layer 6 AI, 2025) — Direct precedent: one transformer trained on simulated ignorable DGPs estimates ATEs/CATEs in-context on new observational data, SOTA on IHDP/ACIC/Lalonde and competitive on real uplift tasks — validates that amortized causal effect estimation transfers to real data.
- Robertson et al., 'Do-PFN: In-Context Learning for Causal Effect Estimation', arXiv:2506.06039, NeurIPS 2025 spotlight — Shows PFNs pretrained on SCMs WITH simulated interventions predict interventional outcomes from observational context without knowing the causal graph — the template for encoding the change/mechanism as an intervention token.
- Azevedo, Deng, Montiel Olea, Rao, Weyl, 'A/B Testing with Fat Tails', Journal of Political Economy 128(12), 2020 — Measured the true effect-size distribution across Bing's experiment platform (extreme fat tails; top 2% of ideas = 74.8% of gains): the empirical prior for tier-2 exhaust shrinkage, and independent support for the 80/20 abstain-tail thesis and the triage economics.
- Grinsztajn, Oyallon, Varoquaux, 'Why do tree-based models still outperform deep learning on tabular data?', NeurIPS 2022 — Pins where GBMs beat any LLM/transformer: medium-size per-customer tabular fitting — so tier-3 response surfaces stay GBM; the transformer earns its place only for cross-dataset amortization/transfer (and the LLM only at the language interface).
- Salganik et al., 'Measuring the predictability of life outcomes with a scientific mass collaboration', PNAS 2020 (Fragile Families) — The irreducibility ceiling the proposal is designed around: predict aggregates, conformalize confidence, ship abstain as a first-class output.
- Angelopoulos, Bates et al., 'Conformal Risk Control' (2022/ICLR 2024) + selective prediction literature — The distribution-free machinery that turns the B-PFN posterior into a sellable CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS/ABSTAIN gate with a guaranteed error rate on the confident subset, calibrated on the sealed real-experiment set.
- Shao et al., 'DeepSeekMath' (GRPO, 2024) + Thinking Machines × Bridgewater 'Learning to Replicate Expert Judgment' (2026) — The lineage lesson honored, not copied: RLVR needs a verifiable reward at scale; here the reward is manufactured (synthetic DGPs + Senu measured outcomes) and consumed by supervised amortized training first — GRPO on an agentic model stays gated behind the PFN showing learnable signal, per the finetuned-readout sequencing.

### RED TEAM — verdict: **REVISE**
Fatal flaws:
- THE ANTISYMMETRIC-HEAD 'PROVABLE KILL' IS FALSE. Arm-swap symmetry makes only a CONSTANT/MARGINAL directional prior unrepresentable (E[s]=0 under arm exchange). It does nothing against CONTENT-CONDITIONAL bias: a head that learns 'arm containing the new/added/feature-rich description ⇒ up' is perfectly antisymmetric (swap arms and the sign dutifully flips) yet exactly reproduces the production engine's always-predicts-lift failure. If the training corpus (synthetic DGPs + Senu triples + exhaust) has any base-rate imbalance in which arm wins conditional on change-type features — and A/B exhaust famously does — the model learns conditional optimism straight through the head. The proposal's strongest theoretical claim ('not a mitigation, a provable kill') is an overclaim a buyer's DS team will falsify with one balanced-flip audit. The prereg cell (C) tests arm-swap flip rate, which the architecture passes by construction — it does not test conditional bias, which is the actual disproven failure mode.
- THE TIER-1 WIN CONDITION IS A STRAWMAN PLUS A CONTAMINATED EVAL. The pre-registered comparison is B-PFN-given-segment-tables vs 'LLM 0.00' — but the LLM's 0.00 was measured on WORDS-ONLY narratives (AMBDOM deliberately withheld the numbers; the readout says 'words-only capped at chance by construction'). The honest tier-1 baselines are (a) a frontier LLM handed the SAME segment tables and (b) a deterministic Simpson-decomposition calculator — because if composition-driven flips truly are 'arithmetic over aggregate ratios,' as the proposal itself says, a ~50-line calculator wired to the existing LLM-translator solves them with no 30M-param model, no training corpus, and no sim2real risk. Either the information is in the aggregates (calculator wins, PFN is unnecessary complexity) or it is not (the PFN's answer is its prior wearing a posterior costume). Simultaneously, experiment (A) trains on DGPs from a generator 'extending AMBDOM' and scores on the 12 AMBDOM cells — train and test from the same generator family, which failure-mode #5 admits is leakage but the prereg does not fix. A 10/12 pass would be close to tautological; only cell (B), n=1 Cookie Cats, is honest evidence.
- THE WATCHDOG CANNOT RUN AT THE TIERS BEING SOLD. The negative-control guard — the single most validated trust asset, and the thing that failed even WITH full data on a hard confounded benchmark — needs per-user rows or at minimum the NC outcome series to compute anything. At tier 0/1 there is nothing for it to chew on, and the LLM-produced mechanism spec m (mediator, sibling, NC, confounders) becomes unverifiable conditioning input: a wrong mediator from the translator flows into a confident, narrow posterior with no gate. GNCE validated the translator only when the mediator's presence in the data could be checked; the swipe-delete abstain boundary was DATA-triggered. So the entire trust story at the cheap rungs collapses to 'the PFN posterior is calibrated' — which PFN theory guarantees only under the sampled prior (Müller et al.'s KL result is in-prior; it says nothing about honest widening off-prior). The proposal repeatedly invokes 'the watchdog stays as gate' at exactly the tiers where the watchdog is architecturally blind.
- OFF-PRIOR CONFIDENT-WRONG IS THE PRODUCT SURFACE, NOT A FOOTNOTE. For a NOVEL change, tier-0/1 customer aggregates describe the CURRENT state; the response curve (how much the mediator shifts, how the outcome responds) is not in that data at any model quality — the proposal's own failure-mode #2 concedes this for mediator-driven flips. So every cheap-tier verdict is prior-dominated: the answer comes from synthetic-taxonomy + Senu base rates. But the sellable slice is counter-intuitive cases, which are off-base-rate BY DEFINITION — the exact regime where a Bayesian-under-its-own-prior model is confidently wrong and where the 'OOD flag routes to abstain' mitigation is an unvalidated heuristic. This is finding #5 (tracks intuition, not truth) re-armed with better math: the intuition is now the training corpus's intuition. The tier-0 '~0.60 at 20–30% coverage' and tier-1 '0.75–0.85 at 40–50%' numbers are invented — no experiment anywhere in the stack supports them.
- CONFORMAL GUARANTEE THEATER + COLD-START ASSETS THAT DON'T EXIST. 'Distribution-free CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS with a guaranteed error rate calibrated on the sealed real-experiment set' — that set is ~6–12 wildly heterogeneous experiments (a mobile game, an email RCT, one scrubbed design partner), across which exchangeability with a new customer's pricing change is indefensible; conformal validity is vacuous at that n and heterogeneity. Diligence kill in one question. Likewise the pretraining corpus lists 'cross-customer A/B exhaust' as pillar (3) — Sidira has one confidential design partner whose specifics were scrubbed at their request; the exhaust pillar is a chicken-and-egg asset the proposal spends before acquiring.
- LITERATURE IS REAL BUT IS ANALOGY, NOT PRECEDENT. Verified: CausalPFN (2506.07918) and Do-PFN (2506.06039) exist as described. But both condition on FULL ROW-LEVEL observational datasets and are evaluated on semi-synthetic benchmarks (IHDP's simulated outcomes are notoriously unreliable as a yardstick; Lalonde is tiny); CausalPFN additionally assumes ignorability. TabPFN's Nature result is row-level supervised prediction ≤10k rows. NOBODY has shown a PFN doing interventional SIGN inference from aggregate/moment tokens — the tier-0/1 conditioning regime that carries the entire down-the-ladder thesis is the one regime with zero precedent. That doesn't kill the idea; it means the proposal's confidence intervals on itself are borrowed from papers that tested something easier.

Evidence collisions: Clean against #1 (no role-play anywhere in the verdict path — genuine), #3 (no LLM ensembling; disagreement-only siren polarity correctly retained), and #6 (aggregate-level outputs, abstain as output class). Three partial collisions the defenses do NOT fully hold against: (#4/#5) the antisymmetric head blocks only marginal bias, so corpus base-rate imbalance re-imports conditional always-predicts-lift — the production blind-test failure mode returns wearing a from-scratch transformer; the proposal's 'mathematically unrepresentable' defense is simply wrong as stated. (#2) magnitude-blindness: at tier 0 the needed magnitudes are absent from the conditioning data, so the model substitutes its prior — structurally identical to the LLM substituting a degenerate prior for magnitude reasoning, just with a trained prior instead of a pretrained one; whether the posterior widens honestly off-prior is asserted, not derivable from PFN theory, and cell (D) (permuted aggregates → abstain) tests in-family noise, not off-prior novel mechanisms. (#5) would it have passed the five production blind tests? Case-5's lesson was that the wedge is gross-vs-net UNIT ECONOMICS + estimability in the partner's own data — at tier 0/1 those magnitudes are not in persona aggregates unless the exact right cross-tab was requested, so B-PFN at the cheap tiers would likely have emitted prior-driven verdicts on several of those cases rather than the abstains that were the correct answer. Also note the AMBDOM 'vs LLM 0.00' anchor is being reused out of regime: that number was words-only; it cannot serve as the baseline for a numbers-in-context method.

Data-ask honesty: Partially real, partially relabeled, partially displaced. REAL: the EVOI mechanism ('send this one table and cases 3/7/12 become callable') is a genuine product insight that converts a scary bulk ask into an incremental ladder — arguably the best single idea in the proposal, and it doesn't even require the PFN (any posterior-bearing estimator supports EVOI). RELABELED: tier-1 'segment tables/moments' sounds cheap but the composition flips need specific cross-tabs (segment × mediator × guardrail metric), and producing a bespoke cross-tab per hypothesis costs a customer data team roughly what an event-level export costs — sometimes more, because Sidira must specify a new schema each time; unless ONE fixed standard aggregate schema is defined, the tier-1 ask is the feature-store ask in installments. DISPLACED: the expensive data doesn't disappear, it moves onto Sidira's balance sheet — the ₹20-lakh Senu factory (now load-bearing for the PRIOR, so its external-validity gap — Indian panel game-task respondents vs a SaaS customer's users — becomes a training-distribution defect, not just an eval caveat), a multi-month training program, and 'cross-customer exhaust' that requires already having the customers the cheap tiers were supposed to win. Tier-2 also quietly assumes customers have well-logged historical A/B episodes with per-segment outcomes, itself a nontrivial ask. Net: the ask is restructured and better sequenced, not removed — which is defensible if said plainly, and the proposal doesn't quite say it.

**Strongest version (repair):** Keep the amortized-prior bet — it is the only proposal class that attacks the data ladder rather than denying it, the verdict path is genuinely non-LLM, and the spike is nearly free. Repair in this order: (1) RUN THE ARITHMETIC-SUFFICIENCY TEST FIRST (≈2 days, ₹0): LLM-translator → mechanism spec → deterministic Simpson/composition CALCULATOR on tier-1 tables, scored on the composition-driven flip cells and aggregate-collapsed Cookie Cats. If the calculator wins, SHIP THAT as the tier-1 wedge in weeks — the PFN then has to earn its place only where the calculator can't go (tier-0 uncertainty, cross-customer transfer, EVOI), a far smaller and honest claim. (2) Fix the spike's eval: train on 5 of the 6 taxonomy families, test on the held-out 6th, PLUS a generator authored independently (different person/model, sealed); baselines = same-tables LLM and the calculator, never words-only 0.00; real cases (Cookie Cats, Hillstrom) are the only promotable evidence, everything synthetic is engineering signal. (3) Demote the antisymmetry claim from 'provable kill' to 'marginal-bias removal' and add a pre-registered CONDITIONAL-bias audit: directional base rate conditional on change-type features on a balanced held-out flip set, with a hard fail threshold. (4) Tier-honest trust surfaces: CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS stamps exist ONLY at tiers 2–3 where the negative-control watchdog can actually compute; tier-0/1 output is capped at posterior-ordered backlog triage + EVOI roadmap, labeled 'unverified — prioritization, not verdict' (this matches the readout's own §5e honesty rules). (5) Conformal calibration per-customer on their own exhaust only; no global guaranteed-error-rate claims until dozens of real labels per stratum exist — until then posterior width is sold as a heuristic, in writing. (6) Define ONE standardized tier-1 aggregate schema (a fixed segment × metric × mediator cross-tab spec) so the tier-1 ask is genuinely a single cheap export, not a bespoke negotiation per hypothesis. (7) Keep the Senu gate exactly as proposed: corpus spend conditions on the spike surviving family-held-out eval AND aggregate-collapsed Cookie Cats; if (A)-repaired fails, the feature-store ask is proven irreducible for ₹0 — which is itself the valuable answer. Sequenced this way, the proposal degrades gracefully: worst case you shipped a calculator-plus-EVOI tier-1 product and killed a bad bet cheaply; best case the PFN adds transfer and tier-0 coverage on top of an already-shipping wedge.

## [calibration-uncertainty] The Certified Skip List: Conformal Selective Prediction + Prediction-Powered Inference over the Hypothesis Backlog

**One-liner:** Stop trying to make the predictor smarter and make the trust a finite-sample statistical guarantee instead: wrap whatever predictor each data tier affords (LLM translator at tier 0, partial-identification bounds at tier 1, grounded g-formula at tier 3) in a conformal abstain gate calibrated on labeled A/B triples (customer exhaust + Senu factory), use prediction-powered inference to certify backlog-level skip/test decisions from a handful of real experiments, and monitor the guarantee with anytime-valid e-processes so every test the customer runs anyway continuously re-certifies the instrument — the abstain boundary, with its live certificate, IS the product.

### Mechanism
The unit is a hypothesis case: (product change description, target metric, customer). The predictor stack — grounded g-formula where feature data exists, else the LLM-translated mechanism spec with whatever computation the tier supports — emits a signed direction score plus trust features: negative-control-guard verdict, mechanism-observability, overlap/consensus diagnostics, the 3-model heterogeneous-bias disagreement flag (used ONLY as an abstain feature, per the measured 0/12 agreement result), and taxonomy bucket (cannibalization, trade-down, gross-vs-net, ...).

Layer 1 — Conformal selective gate. A calibration set of labeled triples (case, predicted direction+trust features, measured truth) comes from three taps: customer A/B exhaust (tier 2), Senu-manufactured triples over-sampling the counter-intuitive taxonomy, and public RCTs. Labels are gated by the watchdog (only trustworthy measured truths enter; a pre-registered ROPE band defines "null" as a third class). Using Learn-then-Test / conformal risk control, pick an abstain threshold on a composite confidence score such that the directional error rate among NON-abstained calls is ≤ α (e.g. 10%) with finite-sample, distribution-free validity. Calibration is Mondrian (bucket-conditional) so the guarantee holds per failure-mode class, not just on average. When calibration data must transfer across customers (tiers 0–1), use weighted conformal prediction under covariate shift with likelihood-ratio weights estimated from whatever aggregates the customer provides — the guarantee degrades to assumption-carrying and is labeled as such.

Layer 2 — Prediction-powered inference over the backlog. The predictor scores all N (~40–200) backlog hypotheses cheaply; the customer runs only k (~5–15) real tests, chosen by active-inference sampling (label where the predictor is least certain). PPI++ then yields valid confidence intervals on the decision quantities that drive ROI — fraction of the backlog that is net-negative, per-segment aggregate effects, ranking quality — at a fraction of the labels, with graceful degradation to the classical k-label estimate if the predictor is worthless (the λ interpolation guarantees PPI never hurts).

Layer 3 — Anytime-valid self-audit. Every certified call later resolved by a real experiment becomes a Bernoulli observation; an e-process bets against "error rate ≤ α". Because e-processes are valid under optional stopping, the certificate page can show the running e-value continuously; crossing 1/δ auto-widens the abstain region and notifies the customer. Every A/B the customer runs anyway doubles as calibration data (appended to the calibration set) and as audit data (fed to the e-process).

Product surface: the Certified Skip List — "these 23 hypotheses carry a ≤10% directional-error certificate (skip/ship), these 9 we abstain on (test them), here is the live e-value showing the certificate has survived 14 sequential checks." Direction + guarantee, never a point estimate — exactly the deliverable constraint.

### Data ladder
- **tier0_persona_only:** No customer labels, no customer-specific guarantee — say so. Deliverables: (a) triage ORDERING of the backlog (no coverage claim); (b) the flip-siren — the 3-model heterogeneous-bias disagreement panel that flagged 100% of magnitude flips, sold purely as an abstain/test-this signal; (c) transferred coverage from Senu + public calibration via weighted conformal with persona-aggregate-estimated shift weights, expected to certify only the dominance-obvious buckets and abstain near-totally on counter-intuitive ones (which is correct behavior, not failure); (d) a quantified 'what tier-2 unlocks' report — the concrete onboarding hook. Expected certified (non-abstained) fraction: ~0–15%, obvious-case buckets only.
- **tier1_aggregates:** Aggregates buy two things. (1) Partial identification: from segment tables and moments, compute Manski/Fréchet–Hoeffding-style bounds on the effect; certify a sign only when the entire bound set is one-signed — a small but genuinely guaranteed slice (mostly one-sided changes), no per-user data needed. (2) Sharper transfer: aggregates estimate the covariate-shift likelihood ratio, tightening weighted-conformal transfer of Senu/public calibration. Expected certified fraction ~15–30%, still ~0 on true magnitude flips (bounds straddle zero there by construction — the honest outcome).
- **tier2_ab_exhaust:** THE PIVOTAL RUNG, and the core onboarding claim: an experiment log (n past A/Bs with summaries — NOT per-user data) is n calibration labels. Split-conformal coverage resolution is 1/(n+1): n=19 experiments already supports a 90%-coverage threshold; n≈40–60 supports usable per-bucket (Mondrian) calibration. The guarantee becomes customer-specific; PPI over the backlog becomes customer-grounded, with active inference telling them WHICH 5–10 tests to actually run for maximum certificate value. Expected certified fraction ~30–50% overall, still low on flips (the words-only predictor's scores can't separate them — the gate correctly abstains). Time-to-first-value: days, because A/B exhaust is a spreadsheet export, not a data-engineering project.
- **tier3_event_level:** The full feature store upgrades the PREDICTOR inside the same wrapper — g-formula mediation sim whose confidence scores actually separate magnitude-flip cases (measured 1.00 vs 0.00). Tier 3 buys COVERAGE/EFFICIENCY, not validity: the certified fraction jumps, including on the counter-intuitive slice where the ROI is concentrated (each caught net-negative pays for the product). Expected certified fraction ~50–70% overall with meaningful flip-slice coverage, abstain-tail preserved. The sales story writes itself: the guarantee is IDENTICAL at every tier — climbing the ladder only buys more non-abstain answers, so customers start at tier 2 friction-free and upgrade when the abstention report shows them what tier 3 unlocks.

### Why it survives the negative results
(1) Persona role-play at 0.29: this proposal contains no persona predictor. Mechanically, a 0.29-accurate predictor entering the wrapper gets its confidence scores calibrated against labeled truths and receives near-total abstention — the system would have MEASURED 0.29 and refused to certify, which is precisely the failure the design partner blind tests exposed in production. (2) Naive LLM at chance: same logic — the wrapper is predictor-agnostic and never claims a predictor works; PPI++'s λ-interpolation formally guarantees a chance predictor cannot make backlog inference worse than the classical k-label estimate. (3) Council-of-priors made it worse: the disagreement panel enters ONLY as an abstain feature with polarity LEARNED from calibration labels (Learn-then-Test optimizes over threshold/feature combinations against measured risk), never as a vote; the measured trigger-polarity inversion (agreement = confidently-wrong) is exactly what data-driven threshold selection encodes and what a hand-designed voting rule got backwards. (4) Model strength doesn't fix magnitude-blindness: accepted as a hard floor — at tiers 0–2 the words-only predictor's scores cannot separate flip cases, so a valid conformal gate MUST abstain there, and the finite-sample guarantee prevents ever selling chance as signal; flip-slice coverage is bought only by the tier-3 grounded predictor, consistent with AMBDOM. (5) Production engine tracks intuition: this is the direct fix — no verdict ships without a finite-sample error certificate plus a live e-process audit that would have flagged 'wrong on every counter-intuitive case' within a handful of resolved tests and auto-widened abstention. (6) Fragile-Families ceiling: selective prediction is the mathematically correct response to a partially-unpredictable domain — it does not deny the ceiling, it PRICES it: the coverage/abstention curve at guaranteed error α is a measurement of where the predictable slice ends, turning the founder's 80/20 bet from a belief into an estimand.

### Testable experiment
Two-part sealed pre-registration, existing assets only, ~10 days, same self-blinding runstore discipline as research/engine-strength-campaign. PART A (does the gate deliver valid selective coverage?): pool every labeled case already on disk — AMBDOM/GNCE synthetic battery cells, Hillstrom, Cookie Cats, the 14-case set, portfolio-benchmark cases (~50–70 total). Predictor stack = existing grounded sim where feature data exists + LLM direction + trust features (3-model disagreement flag, NC-guard verdict, mechanism-observability). Pre-register α=0.10 and the score family; leave-one-bucket-out Mondrian calibration; SEAL thresholds (fingerprint) before scoring held-out buckets. Success: (i) empirical directional error among non-abstained ≤ α (95% binomial-consistent); (ii) non-abstained fraction ≥30% with the grounded predictor, including >0 flip-slice coverage; (iii) the falsification arm — swap in the words-only LLM predictor and the gate must drive flip-slice certification to ~0 (a wrapper that certifies a known-chance predictor is broken and kills the proposal). PART B (does PPI buy label efficiency?): simulate a 100-hypothesis backlog from the synthetic factory; k=10 labels via active sampling; compare PPI++ CI width on 'fraction net-negative' vs the classical 10-label CI over 1,000 resamples. Success: ≥30% CI shrinkage with maintained nominal coverage. Kill criteria: coverage violated in A, or grounded-predictor abstention ≈100%, or PPI shrinkage <10%.

### Failure modes
(1) Small-n vacuity: with <20 calibration labels the guarantee holds but abstention is near-total — valid yet commercially empty; per-bucket Mondrian guarantees need n≈40–60, which is exactly what Senu must supply for thin buckets. (2) Exchangeability breaks: genuinely novel change-types and product/user-base drift violate the assumption; adaptive conformal + the e-process catch violations only AFTER some certified errors ship — the certificate is honest ex post, not clairvoyant. (3) Noisy labels: A/B 'truths' are themselves estimates; underpowered tests near the ROPE boundary inject label noise into calibration — mitigate by watchdog-gating labels and weighting by label precision, but this shrinks the calibration set further. (4) Selection bias in exhaust: customers only ran tests they were unsure about, so calibration skews toward hard cases (conservative for coverage, but distorts score distributions and abstain placement). (5) Marginal-not-conditional guarantee: α is an average over the certified set; a customer may over-read it as per-case probability — the certificate UI must say 'of every 10 skip-calls, ≤1 wrong on average', and bucket-conditioning only partially closes this. (6) Tier-1 disappointment: Manski/Fréchet bounds from aggregates are usually wide, so the tier-1 certified slice may be tiny — fine scientifically, risky for mid-ladder pricing. (7) Senu external validity: transfer weights assume Senu respondents overlap customer users in feature space; if not, weighted conformal weights explode (variance inflation) and tier-0/1 transfer must be disabled rather than fudged. (8) Gaming/feedback: once skip-lists drive which tests run, resolved-label arrival becomes policy-dependent; the e-process tolerates optional stopping but the calibration pipeline must log the selection rule to stay honest.

### Buildability

## [minimal-data-ask] The Sufficient-Statistic Query Compiler: a VOI-ranked data ladder that runs the grounded g-formula from segment moment tables, not row-level event data

**One-liner:** Don't ask for the customer's feature store — compile each hypothesis into a tiny compute-to-data SQL that returns only per-segment moment tables (the minimal sufficient statistic for a directional g-formula verdict), so the surviving grounded-simulation accuracy is delivered at Tier 1 (aggregates) instead of Tier 3 (event-level), with Manski bounds enforcing honest abstain and VOI ranking the next column to ask for.

### Mechanism
Core claim: the surviving engine (grounded mediation g-formula + negative-control guard) does NOT actually need row-level event data to return a DIRECTIONAL verdict on the linear/GLM-mechanism slice. For a partially-linear structural model, the sufficient statistic for the coefficients is the per-stratum Gram matrix (X'X, X'Y) plus cell counts; the g-formula mediation estimate is a closed-form function of those coefficients, the LLM-declared mediator shift, and the covariate moments — all recoverable from segment-level aggregates. One-shot federated ridge regression proves EXACT coefficient recovery from shared sufficient statistics; two-sample summary-data Mendelian randomization proves causal-effect estimation from summary stats alone. We port that fact into product onboarding.

Pipeline, per hypothesis: (1) LLM TRANSLATOR (unchanged from the survivor architecture — never a predictor) turns the English/Figma change into a mechanism spec: mediator, sibling/cannibalization metric, negative control(s), confounders, segmentation, estimand. (2) QUERY COMPILER turns that spec into a small parameterized SQL — a template that GROUP BYs the declared strata and returns, per segment: N, and the means/second-moments (covariances) of {treatment indicator, mediator, outcome, confounders, negative control}. This is a few KB. The customer runs it inside their own warehouse (Snowflake/BigQuery); nothing row-level leaves — compute-to-data, privacy-preserving by construction, no data-sharing agreement, no ML pipeline to install. (3) The refactored causal_estimator consumes those moment tables (instead of a DataFrame), fits the partially-linear response surface from the sufficient statistics, applies the mechanism shift per segment, aggregates to the population sign. (4) NEGATIVE-CONTROL GUARD runs on the same moments (the NC's moment is in the table) → CONFIRMS / CONTRADICTS / CAN'T-VALIDATE. (5) PARTIAL IDENTIFICATION: when the moments only BOUND the effect (unmodeled within-segment heterogeneity, coarse strata), we return Manski-style worst-case bounds on the sign; if the interval straddles zero → honest abstain, not a guess.

The VOI layer is the onboarding ratchet. Each candidate additional column/finer stratification has an expected-information-gain: how much it shrinks the sign-bound (or the probability the g-formula sign flips). We surface the single highest-VOI incremental ask — "add refunds_30d and we can also check cannibalization" / "split by tenure and this abstain resolves." Every answered question tightens the bound and moves the customer up the ladder painlessly, one sufficient-statistic at a time, never "give us your feature store."

Two methods pull the OUTCOME ask down too: the SURROGATE INDEX lets the customer return short-horizon proxies they already log (D1/D7 engagement) in place of the long-horizon business metric, under Prentice surrogacy — validated using their own A/B exhaust. TRANSPORTABILITY/data-fusion pushes the functional-form and negative-control calibration burden onto the Senu factory + public RCTs (Sidira-side, one-time), so the per-customer ask stays minimal; the selection diagram makes explicit which parameters must be re-measured in the customer's population vs. transported.

Net: the same directional accuracy the grounded sim proved at Tier 3 is delivered from a Tier-1 aggregate ask for the mechanism-linear slice — which the 80/20 taxonomy says is most cases — with Tier 3 reserved only for non-linear/interaction-heavy mediation and proximal double-negative-control confounding.

### Data ladder
- **tier0_persona_only:** No causal identification is possible from persona aggregates — this is exactly the disproven rung. Value is strictly non-predictive: (a) LLM translator generates the mechanism spec and the VOI-ranked ask list to CLIMB off this rung; (b) transported Senu-factory reference-class prior gives a base rate for the failure-mode taxonomy; (c) the 3-model heterogeneous-bias council runs ONLY as an abstain siren (agreement=confident-wrong). Directional accuracy on counter-intuitive cases stays ~0.50 → the honest output is 'test it' plus a triage/prioritization ranking of the backlog. We do not sell a prediction here.
- **tier1_aggregates:** The wedge. One compiled compute-to-data SQL returns per-segment moment tables (~KB, nothing row-level leaves). These are the sufficient statistic for a partially-linear g-formula mediation verdict: recovers the magnitude-driven net-negative (the Cookie Cats-class flip) on the mechanism-linear slice — target parity with the Tier-3 directional verdict on the cases whose mechanism is captured by the declared strata (the AMBDOM 1.00-vs-0.00 regime), with Manski-bound abstain everywhere the moments only bound the sign. This is the method that moves the surviving accuracy DOWN from event-level to aggregate. Negative-control guard runs on the same table. Onboarding cost: run a SQL, no data egress, no agreement.
- **tier2_ab_exhaust:** Past A/B history = free labels. Uses: (a) validate/calibrate the response-surface functional form and the negative-control guard against realized ground truth (turn the guard from LEAD to promoted per-customer); (b) fit the surrogate index so future decisions read short-horizon proxies instead of waiting on the long-horizon business metric; (c) per-customer transport parameters (which coefficients differ from the Senu base). Shrinks the abstain zone and unlocks trust-stamping. Ask = summary tables of prior experiments, still aggregate.
- **tier3_event_level:** Only invoked when Tier-1 moments provably cannot identify the sign: strong non-linear interactions in the mediation, per-user heterogeneity that dominates the segment mean, or hard naturally-confounded settings needing proximal / double-negative-control estimators (the open route). Highest accuracy on the complex tail, but the biggest ask — so the VOI layer escalates to it case-by-case, for the minority of hypotheses that need it, rather than demanding it from every customer on day one.

### Why it survives the negative results
1) Persona role-play predictor (0.29, below chance): we NEVER seed LLM agents to produce an outcome. The verdict is a closed-form g-formula evaluated on the customer's real segment moments; the LLM is a translator that emits the mechanism spec and the SQL parameters only. Mechanically different: no agent 'becomes' a user. 2) Naive/comparative one-shot LLM (~0.50): the arithmetic that resolves a magnitude-driven flip is executed on the moment table (means/covariances carry magnitude), not on words. Words-only is magnitude-blind by construction — we removed words from the resolution path entirely. 3) Council-of-priors made it worse: the council appears ONLY as the abstain-siren in the Tier-0 fallback branch (disagreement raises caution), never as a predictor — identical to the runtime flow the readout locked; the kill/keep decision is the data computation. 4) Model strength doesn't fix it (llama optimism / Opus pessimism / gpt-5.5 0.00 on flips): the verdict has zero dependence on model strength — swapping the translator LLM cannot change a g-formula computed from moments. Magnitude is read from data, not inferred from a prior of any polarity. 5) Production blind tests always predict lift: the g-formula on real moments CAN and DOES output a decline (it recovered the Cookie Cats negative from real data); it has no optimism prior because it is arithmetic on the customer's own numbers. 6) Fragile-Families/Salganik data ceiling: we do not claim to beat the ceiling — we claim to reach the SAME verdict from a CHEAPER rung (aggregates vs event-level) and to abstain honestly (Manski bounds) exactly where the data cannot identify the sign. The contribution is data-EFFICIENCY of an already-validated estimator, not a new accuracy claim, so it cannot re-run into the ceiling — it explicitly encodes it.

### Testable experiment
Aggregate-vs-event-level parity test, sealed and pre-registered, on batteries and estimator we already have. On (a) the AMBDOM/GNCE synthetic magnitude-flip battery and (b) the real Cookie Cats + Hillstrom datasets, run the grounded g-formula TWICE per case: once on full row-level data (the Tier-3 path already proven at 1.00 / correct-sign), and once on ONLY the compiled segment moment tables (Tier-1: cell counts + per-segment means and covariances of treatment/mediator/outcome/confounders/negative-control, computed from the same data by the query-compiler SQL). Pre-register three numbers before unblinding: (1) directional-agreement rate between Tier-1 and Tier-3 verdicts (target: Tier-1 reproduces the Tier-3 sign on >=90% of the mechanism-linear cases); (2) on the cases where they disagree, does the Manski-bound abstain correctly fire (i.e., Tier-1 says 'can't identify' rather than confidently wrong)? target: abstain covers the disagreements, zero confident-wrong; (3) negative-control guard verdict identical across tiers. Kill criterion: if Tier-1 confidently contradicts Tier-3 on magnitude-flip cases (not just abstains), the sufficient-statistic reduction is lossy for this estimator class and the thesis fails. Fully offline, uses causal_estimator.py + existing batteries, no customer, no spend, <2 weeks.

### Failure modes
1) Non-linearity / unmodeled interactions: segment moments are sufficient only for the partially-linear response surface within-stratum. If the true mediation is strongly non-linear inside a declared segment, moments lose the flip and Tier-1 must abstain (and escalate to Tier-3) — the win is bounded to the linear-mechanism slice (which the 80/20 taxonomy claims is most, but that is itself the bet). 2) Mis-specified strata: the LLM translator picks the segmentation; a wrong/coarse segmentation hides heterogeneity and can produce a confidently-wrong aggregate — mitigated by Manski bounds widening under coarse strata, but only if the guard is calibrated. 3) Confounder not in the moment table: if a confounder wasn't declared/returned, the g-formula is biased and the negative-control guard is the only backstop — same open weakness as the base architecture (guard failed one hard real benchmark; proximal route unshipped). 4) Small-cell noise: rare segments give unstable second moments; needs shrinkage/regularization and honest CIs, else spurious flips. 5) Transport risk: Senu-calibrated functional forms may not hold in the customer population (external validity) — selection diagram makes the assumption explicit but cannot verify it without Tier-2 exhaust. 6) VOI mis-ranking: if the EIG estimate of the 'next column' is itself model-dependent and wrong, we ask for the wrong statistic and burn onboarding trust. 7) Surrogacy violation: short-term proxy breaks Prentice surrogacy → surrogate index gives wrong long-horizon sign; must be validated per-customer, not assumed.

### Buildability
Mostly a refactor plus a small compiler, not new science. Already exists and is reusable: the LLM mechanism-translator (produces mediator/sibling/NC/confounder/segments today), causal_estimator.py with the g-formula + negative-control guard, the AMBDOM/GNCE synthetic batteries, the Cookie Cats/Hillstrom real cases, the Senu factory, and Snowflake MCP access for the compute-to-data pattern. To build: (1) refactor causal_estimator to consume moment tables (Gram-matrix / per-segment sufficient statistics) instead of a DataFrame — a bounded, well-specified change since the sufficient-statistic form of GLM/g-computation is standard; (2) the query compiler: a templated SQL generator keyed off the existing mechanism spec that GROUP BYs the declared strata and returns counts + first/second moments (a few hundred lines, per-warehouse dialect); (3) the Manski-bound + abstain layer on top of the aggregate estimate; (4) the VOI ranker (EIG over candidate columns/strata) — the most research-y piece, but a v1 greedy 'which added covariance most shrinks the sign-bound' is tractable. Rough effort: the parity experiment above is ~1 week (refactor + moment extraction on existing data); a demo-grade query compiler + Manski abstain + a first VOI ask is ~3-5 weeks. Nothing here needs the customer's event-level feature store or a data-sharing agreement, which is the entire point.

On exactly the cases that matter — two-channel trade-offs where the net sign depends on the RATIO of how much each mediator moves — the verdict is now a function of an LLM-supplied magnitude. Tier-3 grounded sim won AMBDOM because shift magnitudes were read from data (observed treated-arm/natural mediator variation); at Tier 1 for a genuinely novel change the mediator delta is not in historical moments — it is the quantity the A/B test exists to measure. Unless the proposal specifies a data-derived source for shift magnitudes at Tier 1, it fails AMBDOM-class cases while claiming its accuracy.
- SUFFICIENCY CLAIM IS FALSE FOR THE ACTUAL VALIDATED ESTIMATOR: causal_estimator.py (the artifact whose accuracy the proposal borrows) uses LogisticRegression for propensity AND for binary outcomes (retention/conversion — the modal product metric), plus a per-row propensity overlap check (support_check, min_overlap=0.1). (X'X, X'Y) is exact only for linear/ridge/Gaussian; logistic needs the full likelihood — the proposal's OWN citation (Ferrando & Sheldon, arXiv:2405.15002) had to build an APPROXIMATE sufficient-statistic objective for logistic. And positivity/overlap diagnostics cannot be run from segment moments at all — the engine-strength campaign's iter0 finding was that overlap-confidence blindness is the failure mode. So 'recover the identical fit, no accuracy loss' is wrong as written; Tier-1 is a linear-probability approximation without the overlap guardrail, on 1-3pp effects where approximation error rivals the effect.
- MANSKI BOUNDS ARE VACUOUS OR DISHONEST HERE: worst-case partial-identification bounds under unobserved WITHIN-segment confounding on a bounded outcome essentially always straddle zero for realistic 1-3pp product effects. So the honest Tier-1 behavior is near-universal abstain (no product at the wedge rung), or the bounds get tightened with modeling assumptions — at which point 'honest abstain' is a dressed-up prior and the VOI ranker is optimizing a fictional objective. The proposal never states the expected Tier-1 abstain rate; that single number decides whether Tier 1 is a product or a demo.
- THE PARITY EXPERIMENT HAS A LEAK THAT BIASES IT TOWARD PASSING: computing the moment tables 'from the same data' as the Tier-3 run quietly hands Tier-1 the data-derived mediator shift and the correct schema mapping that a real Tier-1 customer would not have. As designed it tests moment-sufficiency of the estimator (interesting, narrow), not the actual Tier-1 product condition (novel change, shift magnitude unobserved, translator-chosen strata against a messy warehouse). It can pass while the product claim stays untested.
- MISAPPLIED CORNERSTONE CITATION: two-sample summary-data Mendelian randomization (Zhao et al. 2020) achieves causal identification from summary stats because SNPs are valid INSTRUMENTS by biology. Product changes have no instruments; summary-stats-plus-confounder-adjustment inherits every confounding weakness that summary-stats-plus-instruments escapes. Citing MR as 'the backbone of the Tier-1 claim' is a category error a buyer's DS team will catch in the first pass.
- CIRCULAR SAFETY AT TIER 1: the escalation rule says invoke Tier 3 for 'hard naturally-confounded settings needing proximal/double-NC' — but the NC guard is a LEAD that already FAILED the one hard naturally-confounded real benchmark, and unobserved confounding is precisely what segment moments cannot reveal (iter0). Tier 1 is safe only where confounding is mild, and Tier 1 cannot tell you where that is; the rung's trust rests on a guard variant (moments-based) that has never been validated even to the LEAD level of the row-level one.

Evidence collisions: Collides with #4 (magnitude-blindness): the LLM-declared mediator shift makes the net-sign verdict on two-channel trade-offs a function of word-derived magnitudes — the defense ('magnitude is read from data, not inferred') holds only for the response-surface slopes, not the shift ratio, so it would FAIL AMBDOM at true Tier-1 conditions even though it would pass the leaky parity replay. Partially collides with #5 (production blind tests): the translator choosing mediator/sibling/NC/strata is the same intuition-shaped LLM that made the deployed engine track intuition; Case-5's lesson (edge = gross-vs-net economics + estimability) means the verdict hinges on the translator remembering to declare refunds/costs — a wrong or optimistic spec produces a confidently-wrong aggregate, and Manski widening only saves you if the guard is calibrated, which it is not (LEAD, one real failure). Avoids #1/#2 cleanly (verdict is arithmetic; no agent role-play, no words in the resolution path for slopes). Consistent with #3 (council appears only as the Tier-0 abstain siren, matching the locked runtime flow). Consistent with #6 (claims data-efficiency of an existing estimator, not new accuracy) — though flaw 2 shows it is not actually the same estimator, so the 'cannot re-run into the ceiling' defense is weaker than stated. GNCE's own boundary (abstain when mediator-proxy unobserved) is respected in spirit but the proposal never says where the Tier-1 shift magnitude comes from, which is the same boundary in a new costume.

Data-ask honesty: Partially honest, partially a relabel. GENUINE reduction: data EGRESS, privacy/legal surface, and the data-sharing agreement — compute-to-data with KB-scale aggregate returns is a real onboarding win (though 'no agreement needed' is optimistic: enterprise legal treats per-segment covariance exports as data sharing, and small cells leak; expect a lighter review, not zero). NOT reduced, only displaced: the customer must still POSSESS a Tier-3-grade per-user feature store — mediator, sibling, negative-control, confounder, segment columns materialized, clean, and correctly mapped to the LLM's mechanism spec — or the compiled SQL has nothing to GROUP BY. The feature-engineering and schema-mapping burden (the actual time-to-first-value friction) is untouched, and the operational ask arguably WORSENS: one vendor-authored SQL per hypothesis per VOI iteration, each needing a customer data/security human to review and run, turns 'adaptive VOI ratchet' into a slow human-in-the-loop email thread. Further displacement onto Senu/public RCTs for functional-form transport is admitted but unverifiable without Tier-2 exhaust. Honest restatement: this is a Tier-3-possession / Tier-1-transmission architecture — a privacy-and-trust ladder, not a data-efficiency ladder. That is still worth building; it is not what the title claims.

**Strongest version (repair):** Reframe as the ZERO-EGRESS DEPLOYMENT MODE of the validated Tier-3 engine, plus an honest ladder that starts at Tier 2. Concretely: (1) Ship compute-to-data as a warehouse-native app (Snowflake Native App / BigQuery routine) that runs the FULL row-level causal_estimator — logistic models, per-row propensity overlap check, NC guard — inside the customer's warehouse and returns only the verdict, the guard stamp, and the moment tables. This captures 100% of the privacy/egress/DPA win with 0% of the sufficiency loss and no unvalidated estimator variant; the moment-table SQL compiler survives only as a degraded fallback for customers who refuse any install, clearly labeled lower-trust. (2) Hard rule: mediator shift magnitudes must be DATA-DERIVED (observed natural variation, past A/B exhaust, or Senu-transported with an explicit selection-diagram transport test) — never LLM-declared numbers; if no data source exists for the shift ratio on a two-channel trade-off, the mandatory output is abstain. This is the one rule that keeps the proposal from re-importing magnitude-blindness. (3) Fix the parity experiment's leak before running it: pre-register a Tier-1-realistic arm where the shift must come only from Tier-1-available information, add logistic-vs-LPM sensitivity and a moments-only overlap-diagnostic stress test, and add a fourth pre-registered number — the Tier-1 abstain RATE. Kill criterion stays; add: if honest Tier-1 abstains on >70% of cases, the wedge rung is Tier 2, not Tier 1. (4) Promote Tier 2 (past A/B exhaust as summary tables) to the headline onboarding rung — randomized exhaust is the only rung that is simultaneously cheap, aggregate, and self-identifying (no confounding assumptions), and it calibrates the guard, the surrogate index, and transport in one ask. (5) Replace Manski worst-case with a sensitivity-analysis abstain (E-value / Rosenbaum-style) calibrated on the Senu factory + Tier-2 exhaust, and keep the greedy VOI-as-bound-shrink v1. (6) Drop the MR citation; keep data-fusion/transportability, surrogate-index, federated-sufficient-statistics (all real and applicable — verified: arXiv 2601.08216 and 2405.15002 exist as described). The parity experiment (repaired) is still the right first move: cheap, offline, existing assets, real kill criterion.

## [simulation-science] Moment-Calibrated Mechanism Simulation (MCMS): simulation-based inference on customer aggregates as the middle rung of the data ladder

**One-liner:** Keep the surviving architecture (LLM = translator, data = computer, watchdog = gate) but replace the per-user feature-store requirement with a parametric micro-simulator whose free behavioral parameters are fitted to the customer's cheap AGGREGATE moments and A/B exhaust via method-of-simulated-moments / simulation-based inference — magnitudes enter from calibration targets instead of per-user rows, direction is issued only when the sign is identified across the fitted posterior, and the identification analysis itself emits the minimal incremental data ask.

### Mechanism
The magnitude-blindness result says the missing information is a small set of NUMBERS (relative channel magnitudes), not per-user detail. The g-formula got those numbers from event-level rows; MCMS gets them from aggregates. Pipeline per hypothesis:

(1) TRANSLATE (exists). The LLM converts the English hypothesis + crawl into a structured mechanism spec: mediator channel(s), cannibalization/sibling channel, segments, confounders, negative-control analog. Same translator role that survived GNCE — it names the structure, never the answer.

(2) COMPILE. The spec is compiled (small DSL) into a parametric micro-simulator: a synthetic population drawn from the persona pack (the WHO — composition, segment shares), each unit governed by simple behavioral response functions (logit/hazard/linear-in-mediator) with a low-dimensional parameter vector θ: channel elasticities, cannibalization share, trade-down propensity, novelty-decay rate, per-segment baselines. The LLM proposes the functional skeleton; θ is left FREE. This is the economics-ABM discipline: agents are fine, ungrounded agents are the sin.

(3) CALIBRATE. Fit θ so the simulator's simulated moments match the customer's observed aggregates — funnel conversion by segment, retention-curve points, revenue/usage mix, engagement quantiles (tier 1), plus each past A/B result as a CAUSAL moment condition (tier 2; observational moments pin baselines and correlations, exhaust pins causal response slopes). Estimation is textbook: method of simulated moments (McFadden 1989) or neural posterior estimation from the SBI literature (Cranmer et al. 2020; `sbi` package), yielding a posterior over θ, not a point.

(4) PREDICT + GATE. Push the proposed change through the calibrated simulator across posterior draws → a distribution over the SIGN of the net effect per metric and segment. Decision rule: issue a direction only if (a) ≥90–95% of posterior mass agrees on the sign, AND (b) the calibrated model reproduces HELD-OUT moments not used in fitting (the aggregate-level analog of the negative-control guard; guards Kennedy–O'Hagan-style misspecification), AND (c) for tier-2 customers, replayed past experiments score above a preregistered backtest bar. Otherwise ABSTAIN — and, crucially, the identification analysis outputs WHY: which specific missing table (e.g., "retention split by spender tier") would collapse the posterior to one sign. The abstain message becomes the incremental data ask, so customers climb the ladder one CSV at a time instead of exporting a feature store on day one.

(5) NARRATE (exists). Segment-level calibrated counterfactuals feed the talk-to-users layer exactly as in §5c of the readout — numbers first, persona agent speaks for them.

Cross-customer prior: a hierarchical empirical-Bayes prior over θ and effect sizes, fit on Senu factory triples + pooled customer exhaust (Azevedo et al. show experiment exhaust supports exactly this), initializes calibration so a new customer starts from the population of businesses, not from an LLM's optimism. Where the ABM genuinely earns its keep over plain algebra: interaction effects, mix-shift/Simpson flips, and marketplace/congestion feedback; for single-channel mechanisms the compiled model degenerates to two lines of algebra — ship that and skip the theater.

### Data ladder
- **tier0_persona_only:** NO verdict, ever (this is the disproven rung as a predictor). Deliverables: population composition for the simulator (the WHO), mechanism-spec generation, and a pre-analysis IDENTIFICATION REPORT — 'here are the 3–5 aggregate tables that would let us call this hypothesis' — plus the 3-model heterogeneous-bias panel strictly as abstain siren. Tier 0's product value is time-to-first-artifact and a concrete, tiny data ask; accuracy value ≈ 0 by construction and we say so.
- **tier1_aggregates:** First rung with verdicts. MSM/SBI calibration to segment-level funnel, retention, and mix moments identifies the sign for hypotheses whose net depends on magnitude ratios that surface in aggregates — expected to cover most single-mediator-plus-cannibalization flips IF segment-split tables are provided (unsplit aggregates are Simpson-blind to mix-flips). Quantitative target from the sealed experiment below: ≥0.85 directional accuracy on CALLED cells with ≤50% abstain on the AMBDOM flip battery (words-only ceiling: 0.0–0.5). Caveat: tier-1 moments are observational, so causal slopes are prior-driven — confidence stays 'moderate', abstain rate is honest and high.
- **tier2_ab_exhaust:** The trust rung. Each past experiment is a causal moment condition that pins response elasticities directly and shrinks the θ-posterior hardest per byte of any data type; exhaust also fits the customer-specific empirical-Bayes effect-size prior (fat-tailed per Azevedo et al.) and powers SEALED BACKTESTS (replay held-out past experiments) — the calibration certificate that makes CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS sellable. Expected: abstain rate drops toward ~20–30%, called-cell accuracy approaches tier-3, and confidence becomes empirically calibrated rather than asserted. ~10–30 past experiments (one CSV of arm/metric/segment results) is a trivial export vs a feature store.
- **tier3_event_level:** The already-proven ceiling: the simulator's response surfaces are fit per-user and MCMS collapses to the grounded g-formula (1.00 on flips, Cookie Cats sign recovered). New role: tier-3 customers become the measurement instrument for the frontier itself — degrade their data to tier-1/2 views and measure the accuracy/abstain delta per rung, turning 'what does each tier buy' into a published number instead of an argument.

### Why it survives the negative results
(1) Persona role-play 0.29: mechanically different — the LLM never produces the outcome and no agent 'becomes' a user. Personas contribute only population composition; behavior comes from parametric response functions whose parameters are FIT TO THE CUSTOMER'S MEASURED MOMENTS. If the moments can't pin the sign, the output is abstain, never an LLM guess. (2) Naive one-shot ~0.50: there is no words-only prediction anywhere in the verdict path; the verdict is a computation over a fitted posterior. (3) Council-of-priors made it worse: no voting anywhere; the panel survives only in its validated abstain-siren role at tier 0 / in the no-data branch — it never touches a verdict. (4) Magnitude-blindness ('the information is not in the words'): MCMS is a direct attack on exactly this — magnitudes enter through numeric calibration targets (moments, exhaust), which is method-of-simulated-moments' entire reason to exist; AMBDOM already proved data-fitted models resolve flips words cannot (1.00 vs 0.00), and MCMS only relaxes WHERE the fitting data comes from (aggregates vs per-user rows), which the sealed experiment tests rather than assumes. (5) Production engine always-predicts-lift: direction here emerges symmetrically from the θ-posterior; the cross-customer prior is fit on Senu triples that over-sample counter-intuitive negatives, and the sign-identification gate abstains when mass straddles zero — structurally incapable of a constant-lift prior. (6) Salganik/Fragile-Families ceiling: MCMS predicts segment/population aggregates only, never individuals; the per-person view remains labeled calibrated fiction; and the binding-constraint-is-data finding is honored by making the abstain rule a function of identification (data sufficiency), not model confidence.

### Testable experiment
Tier-degradation ablation, sealed, on data already on disk (~1–2 weeks, no customer dependency). Cases: the 12 AMBDOM magnitude-flip cells (synthetic, truth known) + real Cookie Cats. For each case, mechanically DEGRADE the per-user data to a tier-1 view (segment-level aggregate moments only: means/quantiles of mediators and outcomes from the baseline arm; per-user rows deleted) and a tier-2 view (tier-1 + 1–3 unrelated past 'experiments' as causal calibration moments). Reuse the existing GNCE translator prompts to produce mechanism specs; compile the parametric simulator; fit θ with MSM or the `sbi` package; PRE-REGISTER the decision rule (≥90% posterior sign mass + held-out-moment reproduction → call; else abstain) and fingerprint predictions before scoring, same runstore discipline as the engine-strength campaign. Success: called-cell directional accuracy ≥0.85 at ≤50% abstain on tier-1 flips (vs words-only 0.0–0.5), tier-2 abstain materially lower, and Cookie Cats' counter-intuitive negative recovered from aggregates alone. Kill criteria: called-cell accuracy ≤ chance, or abstain >80% of cells, or Cookie Cats sign wrong at tier 2 — then the ladder cannot be descended below the feature store and the heavy data ask stands.

### Failure modes
(1) Equifinality / identification failure: many θ reproduce the same aggregates with opposite counterfactual signs → honest but crippling abstain rates at tier 1; the experiment measures this directly. (2) Misspecification: if the LLM's mechanism spec names the wrong mediator or functional form, calibration produces a confidently-wrong model (Kennedy–O'Hagan); held-out moments mitigate, cannot cure — spec review + multiple-spec ensembles needed. (3) Simpson/ecological blindness: unsplit aggregates cannot see mix-flips — exactly the failure taxonomy we care about — so segment-split tables must be a hard requirement at tier 1, raising the 'cheap' ask slightly. (4) Tier-1 moments are observational: causal slopes are prior-driven until exhaust arrives; a confounded customer baseline can tilt calibration (the NC-guard's open proximal problem recurs one level up). (5) Nonstationarity: moments computed pre-change may drift (seasonality, growth) before the counterfactual applies. (6) Theater risk: for single-channel mechanisms the ABM adds nothing over two lines of algebra — must auto-degenerate or it's simulation cosplay. (7) Prior contamination: a weak Senu-fit hierarchical prior could smuggle optimism back in; prior must be validated on held-out real RCTs before it touches any verdict.

### Buildability
Already exists in-repo/stack: persona packs + builds (the WHO), the crawl (current-state grounding), GNCE/AMBDOM translator prompts and synthetic battery with ground truth, causal_estimator.py + negative-control guard, the sealed runstore/LEDGER discipline (research/engine-strength-campaign), and the Senu factory pipeline (#547–#549 end-to-end) for manufacturing prior-fitting triples. Must build: (a) mechanism-spec → parametric-simulator compiler — a small DSL over ~6 response-function templates (logit conversion, hazard retention, linear mediator shift, cannibalization share), ~1–2 weeks; (b) calibration loop — wrap the open-source `sbi` package or plain MSM with scipy, days; (c) sign-identification + abstain layer over the posterior with the held-out-moment gate, ~1 week; (d) moment-ingestion schema (a handful of CSV table formats — the new, tiny onboarding surface). Research prototype capable of running the kill experiment: ~2–3 weeks of focused work. Production wiring afterward is the same §5d rewiring roadmap already planned (translator demoted, computation promoted, guard in front), with MCMS inserted as the middle rung between 'no data' and 'feature store'.

### Literature
- McFadden (1989) 'A Method of Simulated Moments…' Econometrica; Pakes & Pollard (1989) — 35-year-old, solved estimation theory for exactly this move: fit a micro-simulator's parameters to observed AGGREGATE moments. MCMS is MSM with an LLM writing the model skeleton.
- Cranmer, Brehmer & Louppe (2020) 'The frontier of simulation-based inference', PNAS 117(48):30055 — https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1912789117 — Modern neural SBI gives posteriors over simulator parameters from summary statistics when likelihoods are intractable — the calibration engine for step 3, with off-the-shelf tooling (`sbi`).
- Chopra et al., 'On the Limits of Agency in Agent-based Models' (AAMAS 2025; AgentTorch) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10568 — Direct precedent for the division of labor: LLM 'archetypes' supply behavior structure while STRUCTURAL PARAMETERS ARE CALIBRATED to observed aggregate time series (NYC, 8.4M agents) — LLM-as-policy, numbers-from-calibration, at scale.
- Platt (2020) 'A comparison of economic agent-based model calibration methods', JEDC 113 — https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05938 — Benchmarks ABM calibration estimators; Bayesian estimation consistently beats objective-function approaches — dictates the posterior-based (not point-fit) design that the abstain rule needs.
- Kennedy & O'Hagan (2001) 'Bayesian calibration of computer models', JRSS-B — The canonical warning: calibrated models are confidently wrong under misspecification unless a discrepancy term / held-out checks are included — motivates the held-out-moment gate (watchdog analog) and its known identifiability limits.
- Azevedo, Deng, Montiel Olea, Rao & Weyl (2020) 'A/B Testing with Fat Tails', JPE 128(12) — https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/710607 — Empirical-Bayes priors fit from A/B exhaust (Bing) are real and fat-tailed — the formal machinery for tier 2's customer-specific effect-size prior and for the skip/test triage economics.
- Athey, Chetty, Imbens & Kang, 'The Surrogate Index' (REStud 2025; NBER w26463) — https://www.nber.org/papers/w26463 — Rigorous template for predicting an unobserved outcome from observed proxies learned on auxiliary experiments — the logic by which exhaust + short-term moments stand in for the untested outcome.
- Manski, 'Identification for Prediction and Decision' (2007) / partial-identification program — Sign identification under weak data is a first-class object: report the identified SET; call direction only when the set excludes zero — the abstain rule made rigorous rather than vibes-based.

### RED TEAM — verdict: **REVISE**
Fatal flaws:
- TIER-1 IDENTIFICATION IS BROKEN AS SPECIFIED. The experiment defines tier 1 as 'means/quantiles of mediators and outcomes from the baseline arm.' MARGINAL moments of mediator and outcome separately cannot identify the outcome-given-mediator response slope — the joint distribution is unconstrained. Equifinality (failure mode 1) is not a risk to be measured; it is a mathematical certainty under the stated inputs. To pin the slopes that resolve a flip you need JOINT cross-moments (segment × mediator-bin × outcome cross-tabs, covariances) — which only a customer who already has the per-user feature store can compute. The proposal's own quantitative promise (≥0.85 at ≤50% abstain on tier-1) has no identification basis under its own tier-1 definition.
- THE FIRST-STAGE MAGNITUDE IS IN NO TIER OF CUSTOMER DATA — AND IT IS THE NUMBER THAT DECIDES FLIPS. For a NOVEL change, 'how much does removing the cap shift usage / how much cannibalization' is not in baseline aggregates (the change never ran), not in exhaust (unless a near-identical lever was tested), and not in event-level rows either. It enters through the hierarchical prior (Senu triples, other customers) or the LLM's spec. AMBDOM's lesson was that the net sign on flip cells depends on exactly these channel-magnitude RATIOS. So on the hard slice, the verdict path contains prior/words-derived magnitudes — a partial re-collision with the magnitude-blindness result the proposal claims to solve. Tier 3 dodged this only because the mediator's response surface was fit to real per-user variation; MCMS at tier 1 replaces that with a prior and calls the result a posterior.
- THE 90%-POSTERIOR-MASS GATE MANUFACTURES CONFIDENCE FROM THE PRIOR. Under partial identification, a Bayesian posterior in the non-identified directions IS the prior; 90% sign-mass can be achieved with zero data information if the prior leans. The proposal cites Manski but does not implement him — the honest Manski version reports the identified sign SET and abstains whenever the set contains both signs, which at true tier 1 is almost always → honest tier 1 collapses to ~100% abstain, i.e., tier-0 product value with extra machinery. The proposal cannot have both the Manski citation and the tier-1 accuracy promise.
- SPEC UNCERTAINTY IS OUTSIDE THE POSTERIOR AND THE HELD-OUT-MOMENT GATE DOES NOT CATCH IT. The LLM chooses the mediator, the sibling, and the functional skeleton from ~6 templates — words picking the model class that determines which signs are reachable. Kennedy–O'Hagan (correctly cited) says calibrated models are confidently wrong under misspecification; the proposed cure (reproduce held-out OBSERVATIONAL moments) validates fit, not counterfactual response — the Lucas-critique point: many models matching ALL baseline moments diverge under intervention. A single confidently-wrong spec sails through all three gates. Spec-ensembles are mentioned in failure modes but are absent from the decision rule.
- THE KILL EXPERIMENT IS RIGGED TOWARD PASSING. The AMBDOM synthetic cells were generated from simple parametric mechanisms — almost certainly inside (or near) the 6-template DSL's model class, so tier-degraded calibration is a well-specified-case test and a pass will not transfer to real customers whose DGP is not in the DSL. Worse, the experimenters choose WHICH moments survive degradation — moment selection is an identification leak (in production the customer hands over whatever tables exist). Only the Cookie Cats leg is real, and it is n=1. As designed, only a FAILURE of this experiment is informative; a pass proves little.
- SENU-PRIOR EXTERNAL VALIDITY IS LOAD-BEARING, NOT A CAVEAT. The cross-customer prior that fills every identification gap is fit on ~₹40 Tamil Nadu micro-task respondents playing game templates. Transporting channel-elasticity RATIOS from that population to a US SaaS pricing change is exactly the unvalidated transfer the readout flags for Senu-trained models ('transfer is the upside AND the external-validity risk — must be validated, not assumed'). Since flaw 3 shows the prior IS the tier-1 verdict, prior contamination is not failure mode 7 — it is the tier-1 mechanism. This is the road back to a structural constant-direction bias (production finding #5) wearing Bayesian clothes.
- OPERATIONAL: per-hypothesis compile→SBI is fragile in practice (each hypothesis = a new simulator = a new NPE fit or MSM run needing simulation-budget tuning and convergence diagnostics); 'wrap the sbi package, days' underprices this. Non-fatal but the 2–3-week estimate is the optimistic tail.

Evidence collisions: Checked against all 6. (1) Persona role-play 0.29 — genuinely avoided in the letter (no agent produces an outcome), but partially re-entered through the back door: the LLM's mechanism spec + template choice determines the reachable sign set, and the prior supplies the first-stage magnitudes, so on flip cases words/priors still sit inside the verdict path (fatal flaws 2 and 4). (2) Naive one-shot ~0.50 — avoided; no words-only verdict. (3) Council-worse — avoided; panel stays in the abstain-siren role only. (4) Magnitude-blindness — the proposal's central claim is that it attacks this, and at tier 2/3 that is defensible; at tier 1 AS SPECIFIED it would re-run into it, because marginal baseline moments do not carry the channel-ratio information and the gap is filled by prior/LLM structure — MCMS-tier-1 would plausibly have FAILED AMBDOM under an honestly misspecified DSL and an honestly customer-realistic moment menu, and the proposed experiment is constructed so as not to test that (fatal flaw 5). (5) Production always-predicts-lift — structurally avoided (symmetric posterior), but the Senu-fit hierarchical prior is the documented route by which a constant directional bias returns; the proposal's own failure-mode 7 concedes this while the tier-1 accuracy target quietly depends on that same prior. (6) Salganik ceiling — honored (aggregate-only predictions, abstain tied to identification). Net: collisions 1/4/5 are not fully cleared at tier 1; tier 2 (exhaust as causal moment conditions) clears all six for levers similar to previously tested ones, which is where the proposal is actually strong.

Data-ask honesty: Partially honest, partially displacement — in three distinct ways. (a) GENUINE reduction of the TRANSFER/PRIVACY ask: shipping aggregate sufficient statistics instead of per-user rows is a real onboarding win (DPDP/GDPR/security review is often the binding friction, and moment tables dodge it). (b) NO reduction of the INSTRUMENTATION ask: to compute the joint segment×mediator×outcome cross-moments that tier 1 actually needs (fatal flaw 1), the customer must already have the per-user behavioral store and a data team to query it — MCMS changes what leaves the building, not what must exist inside it. A customer who cannot export the feature store because they never built one gets nothing from tier 1. (c) DISPLACEMENT of the causal ask onto Senu and onto trust: the first-stage magnitudes for novel changes come from the Senu/cross-customer prior, i.e., the data ask is moved from the customer to Sidira's own unproven factory (₹20-lakh program, month-4–5 gate not yet passed), and the customer is asked to trust a prior fit on a different population. The abstain-emits-minimal-data-ask mechanism is the most honest piece of the design and genuinely converts the ladder into a product motion — but the tier labeled 'first rung with verdicts' is, under honest identification, mostly an identification-report rung. Tier 2 (10–30 past experiment readouts, one CSV) is the one rung where the cheap-ask claim is fully honest AND causally informative.

**Strongest version (repair):** Rebuild MCMS around tier 2 as the entry rung and demote tier 1 to bounds. Concretely: (1) ENTRY ASK = 'send us your last 10–30 experiment readouts' (arm/metric/segment CSV — trivially exportable, causally informative, no privacy review) + the persona pack. Fit the Azevedo-style customer-specific effect-size prior and the θ response slopes from exhaust as causal moment conditions; sell the SEALED BACKTEST (replay held-out past experiments) as the calibration certificate — this is the rung where CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS becomes sellable and every one of the 6 negative results is cleared for levers within the exhaust's span. Flag out-of-span novel levers explicitly and route them to abstain-or-test. (2) TIER 1 REDEFINED as Manski, not Bayes: Sidira ships 5–10 warehouse-ready SQL snippets; the customer returns JOINT cross-tab aggregates (segment × mediator-bin × outcome) — privacy-preserving sufficient statistics, honestly described as 'requires that you have the events, not that you ship them.' Output = identified sign SETS with prior-free bounds; call a direction only when the set excludes zero under EVERY spec in a mandatory multi-spec ensemble (2–3 LLM-generated mechanism specs must agree on reachable-sign structure); otherwise emit the identification report + minimal incremental ask. Accept that honest tier-1 verdict coverage may be small; its product value is time-to-first-artifact and the data-ask generator, and say so. (3) FIX THE EXPERIMENT so a pass means something: add adversarial cells whose true DGP lies OUTSIDE the 6-template DSL (misspecification arm); pre-commit the degraded-moment menu to a customer-realistic table list authored blind to θ (fingerprinted before any calibration); score tier 1 on the honest Manski abstain rate, not posterior mass; keep the Cookie Cats leg and add Hillstrom as a second real case. Kill criteria as proposed, plus: if the misspecification arm produces confident wrong signs that pass the held-out-moment gate, the gate is dead and spec-ensemble agreement becomes mandatory. (4) QUARANTINE THE PRIOR: the Senu/cross-customer hierarchical prior may initialize calibration and shape ABSTAIN boundaries but may never be the majority of the sign-information in a CALLED verdict — operationalize with a prior-sensitivity check (flat-prior refit must preserve the sign) as a fourth gate; validate the prior on held-out real RCTs before it touches anything, per the proposal's own failure-mode 7. (5) Keep unchanged: translator role, auto-degeneration to algebra for single-channel mechanisms, abstain-as-incremental-data-ask, tier-3 customers as the instrument that measures the per-rung accuracy delta (the single best idea in the proposal — it turns the data-efficiency frontier into a published number). This version survives diligence: every called verdict traces to either the customer's own experiments or prior-free bounds on their own aggregates, and the prior is demonstrably not the verdict.

## [systems-pragmatist] The Backtest Ladder: Warehouse-Native Mechanism Transport, Sold as an Audit That Pays for Its Own Data

**One-liner:** Ship a tier-2 "audit your past A/B wins" product (winner's-curse correction + sealed backtest on data customers already export in an afternoon) as the trust wedge, and use cross-customer mechanism transport onto moment-matched synthetic populations to move the proven g-formula predictor down from per-user feature stores (tier 3) to segment aggregate tables (tier 1) — with a sign-stability gate that abstains exactly where aggregates can't pin the trade-off.

### Mechanism
Four layers, each shippable independently, ordered by product sequencing rather than research elegance.

(1) Zero-friction ingestion. A dbt package / read-only Snowflake share (the repo already speaks Snowflake) computes three standardized artifacts CUSTOMER-SIDE so raw rows never leave: `experiments` (one row per past A/B: arm definitions, dates, primary + guardrail lifts, per-segment breakdowns where logged), `segment_moments` (segment × metric means/variances, mediator means, mediator–outcome correlations — tier 1), and optionally the full per-user store (tier 3). Tier 2 is a CSV every Statsig/Eppo/Optimizely/warehouse shop can produce in an hour.

(2) The wedge product (tier 2, ships first): Experiment Portfolio Audit. Pure computation on their own exhaust, zero prediction claims: empirical-Bayes shrinkage of reported lifts against the program-wide effect distribution (Azevedo et al.; Coey & Cunningham), Lee–Shen winner's-curse correction ("your launched wins are worth ~X% of what the dashboard said; these two are likely net-negative"), gross-vs-net and guardrail re-reads, heterogeneity re-analysis. This is real, precedented value (Airbnb/Microsoft ran this internally) and it is the data grant: the contract includes permission to use their exhaust as labeled cases.

(3) The trust certificate: sealed per-customer backtest. For each past experiment, the LLM (translator only) converts its description into a mechanism spec (mediator, sibling/cannibalization metric, negative control, confounders); the engine predicts direction time-masked (only pre-experiment data), then reveals the measured truth from the exhaust. Output: a per-customer calibration curve and abstain thresholds. Accuracy is measured on their own history before a single forward prediction is shown — evals culture as product.

(4) The data-efficiency research core: mechanism transport + moment-matched substrate. Response surfaces (mediator→outcome curves, cannibalization elasticities) are fit where per-user data exists — tier-3 customers, Senu factory triples, public corpora — and indexed by failure-mode taxonomy class and effect modifiers. Bareinboim–Pearl transportability says the target customer then contributes only P*(effect modifiers): segment tables, not rows. For customers without per-user data, synthesize a pseudo-population from their moments (IPF/copula population synthesis), run the standard g-formula per synthetic user with the transported surface, and deliver a direction ONLY if the sign is stable across an ensemble of dependence structures consistent with those moments. If plausible copulas flip the sign, the honest output is "this trade-off is dependence-sensitive — grant per-user rows or run the test."

Watchdog architecture unchanged: negative-control guard (proximal upgrade path) gates every verdict; precedence trustworthy-empirical > LLM > untrustworthy-empirical; the heterogeneous-bias council survives only as an abstain siren. Flywheel: every test a customer does run auto-ingests as a new labeled (population, change, outcome) triple; Senu over-samples whatever taxonomy cells the library lacks. Kill-list: no persona-LLM prediction, no RL fine-tune before the factory shows learnable signal, no calibrated point estimates, no digital-twin UI, no connector zoo beyond Snowflake + CSV.

### Data ladder
- **tier0_persona_only:** NO directional verdicts — measured chance-or-worse, so don't fake it. Ships anyway, in minutes: mechanism-spec structuring of the hypothesis backlog (mediator/sibling/NC/confounders per idea), failure-mode taxonomy classification, breadth-of-risk checklist (the guardrail metrics the PM forgot), library base rates clearly labeled prior-not-prediction ('changes in this class were net-negative in N% of library cases; dominant failure mode: cannibalization'), and a precise 'data quote' — the exact aggregate tables that would unlock a verdict. Value = structuring + an honest quote; it is the top of the funnel, not a predictor.
- **tier1_aggregates:** Transported g-formula on a moment-matched synthetic population, gated by sign-stability across the dependence-structure ensemble. Expected coverage (to be measured, not asserted): in-taxonomy cases where segment moments pin the trade-off get a confident direction; magnitude-flip cases split — the pre-registered target is ≥9/12 AMBDOM flip cells correct-and-confident with ZERO confident-wrong, remainder abstained. Buys a real predictive product from data a customer can hand over without a DPA fight (no rows leave). The rung lives or dies on the 2-week degradation experiment below.
- **tier2_ab_exhaust:** The recommended entry rung and the commercial wedge. Everything tier 1 buys, plus: (a) immediate stand-alone ROI — winner's-curse/EB audit of the launched portfolio (Airbnb/Microsoft precedent says reported win totals shrink materially); (b) the per-customer sealed backtest calibration certificate — accuracy claims become empirical per customer; (c) an empirical-Bayes prior on their effect-size distribution (fat-tailed per Azevedo et al.) that calibrates abstain thresholds; (d) their exhaust joins the cross-customer library with permission — the flywheel. Time-to-first-value: days, one CSV.
- **tier3_event_level:** The proven ceiling, now sold as an upgrade rather than the entry toll: per-customer g-formula (1.00 vs LLM 0.00 on magnitude-driven flips; recovered the Cookie Cats negative), full heterogeneity/CATE and segment counterfactuals, proximal negative controls for the naturally-confounded cases the current guard fails, and surrogate-index estimation (Athey–Chetty–Imbens–Kang) to predict long-horizon metrics from short-term mediators. Tier-3 customers also become response-surface donors, which is what makes tiers 0–1 work for everyone else.

### Why it survives the negative results
(1) Persona role-play 0.29: no LLM ever produces an outcome here. Personas survive only as narrators of computed records (the readout's §5c rule); every arrow comes from a fitted response surface run through the g-formula. (2) Naive/comparative LLM ~0.50: there is no words-only prediction path anywhere in the ladder — words become mechanism specs; tier 0 explicitly refuses to emit verdicts rather than emitting chance-level ones. (3) Council-of-priors made it worse: the council appears only in the abstain branch as a caution siren triggered by disagreement, exactly the one validated use; it never votes on a verdict. (4) Magnitude-blindness is robust to model strength: this proposal's entire premise agrees — magnitudes must come from data. The novelty is WHERE the data comes from: transported fitted surfaces (donor customers/Senu) supply the response curves, customer moments supply the population; the sign-stability gate abstains precisely when the moments do not pin the trade-off, i.e., it respects the same information-theoretic wall instead of prompting past it. The Hewitt et al. result (LLMs predict survey-experiment ATEs at r≈0.85) marks the boundary cleanly: word-native effects are LLM-predictable, magnitude-native product trade-offs are not — the ladder routes each to the right engine. (5) Production engine tracks intuition, always predicts lift: structurally impossible here — no verdict ships without mechanism coverage + the customer's own backtest calibration + watchdog pass, and the default state is abstain; a constant-optimism prior would fail the sealed backtest certificate on the customer's own exhaust before any forward prediction is shown. (6) Fragile-Families ceiling: all claims are segment-level; per-person counterfactuals stay labeled calibrated fiction; the abstain tail is explicit at every rung, and tier 0/1 abstention is the product behaving correctly, not failing.

### Testable experiment
The Moment-Degradation Battery (~1 week of build on the existing engine-strength-campaign runstore with its self-blinding generator; pre-registered and sealed like every prior battery). Question: does the proven tier-3 g-formula survive being fed only tier-1 artifacts? Procedure, per dataset (AMBDOM's 12 magnitude-flip cells, GNCE, real Cookie Cats, Hillstrom, and the 10 realistic one-sign B2 datasets): (a) strip the per-user data down to the exact dbt-package output spec — segment × arm moments, mediator means, mediator–outcome correlations; (b) rebuild a synthetic population via IPF + a Gaussian-copula ensemble with perturbed dependence; (c) transport the response surface — fit on a different dataset of the same mechanism class where possible (true transfer test), else on the pre-period; (d) run the unchanged g-formula + sign-stability gate; (e) score sign vs sealed truth. Pre-registered win: ≥9/12 AMBDOM flip cells correct-and-confident with ZERO confident-wrong; Cookie Cats' counter-intuitive negative recovered from moments alone; zero confident-wrong across the 10 one-sign datasets (abstain always allowed). Kill condition: any confident-wrong on flips, or <6/12 correct → the tier-1 rung is dead, the minimum honest data ask is tier 2/3, and the ladder collapses to audit-wedge + tier-3 upsell. Either outcome is decision-grade for the product roadmap.

### Failure modes
(1) Dependence misspecification: the copula ensemble may not span the true joint structure, so the sign-stability gate itself could be confidently wrong — the one failure that breaks the brand; the battery's zero-confident-wrong bar exists for exactly this. (2) Transport violation: donor response surfaces (other customers, Senu's ₹40 respondents) may not describe this customer's users; mitigated by the tier-2 backtest (transport is falsified against their own exhaust before any forward verdict) but a customer with thin exhaust gets weaker falsification. (3) Library sparsity / the 80-20 taxonomy being false: if new cases don't fall into seen mechanism classes, tiers 0–1 degrade to abstain-everything — same month-4–5 learning-curve gate as Senu. (4) Exhaust quality: survivorship (only significant experiments logged), missing per-segment breakdowns, drifting metric definitions — the sealed backtest silently overstates calibration if losers were never recorded; require the platform export, not a curated deck. (5) Commercial: the winner's-curse audit can land as an insult ('your wins were fake') or a vitamin — must be framed as found-money re-ranking with a named net-negative catch in the first meeting. (6) Privacy/DPA friction on the cross-customer library — mitigate by pooling only fitted surfaces and aggregates, never rows, with explicit per-customer opt-in. (7) Leakage discipline: a customer's exhaust must be excluded from any library artifact used in their own backtest, and backtests must be strictly time-masked.

### Buildability
Already exists in the repo/stack: Snowflake MCP tools + the feature-store connections epic (warehouse-native read path, PASSWORD auth, health checks), the crawler (current-state grounding), causal_estimator.py with the 3-part negative-control guard, the g-formula/AMBDOM/GNCE batteries and their datasets, the append-only runstore + self-blinding prereg harness (becomes the sealed-backtest engine almost as-is), the LLM-translator mechanism-spec pattern, portfolio-benchmark cases, and the Senu export pipeline (#547–#549) feeding labeled triples. To build: (a) the dbt package / aggregate-export spec — 1–2 weeks, mostly schema design; (b) the EB shrinkage + winner's-curse audit module — 1–2 weeks of standard, well-documented stats; (c) IPF/copula synthesizer + sign-stability ensemble — 2–3 weeks (mature open-source components); (d) mechanism-library schema + transport layer — the research-grade piece, 4–6 weeks, gated on the battery; (e) productized backtest runner — ~2 weeks on top of the prereg harness. Net: the tier-2 wedge is shippable in ~4–6 weeks with zero new research risk; the tier-1 rung is gated on one 2-week kill experiment; tier 3 is the already-validated stack. Nothing on the critical path requires training a model.

### RED TEAM — verdict: **REVISE**
Fatal flaws:
- TARGET-MARKET INVERSION at the entry rung: tier 2 requires rich A/B exhaust, but exhaust-rich customers (Statsig/Eppo/warehouse-mature shops) have DS teams who already know winner's curse — Eppo and Statsig can ship EB shrinkage as a platform feature update on data they already hold, commoditizing the wedge — while the buyer who actually wants skip-the-test prediction (few tests, thin exhaust) has nothing to audit and no backtest to certify. The 'recommended entry rung' structurally excludes the prediction customer and invites platform-native competition for the audit customer.
- THE SIGN-STABILITY GATE ENSEMBLES THE WRONG UNCERTAINTY AXIS: it perturbs copula dependence structures but holds the transported response surface and the change→mediator shift fixed. On magnitude-driven flips the dominant error terms are (a) surface-transport error (donor curve ≠ this customer's curve) and (b) Δmediator error — neither is in the ensemble, so the gate can be confidently wrong through an un-ensembled axis. That is precisely the one brand-breaking failure the proposal itself names, and the pre-registered battery as designed would validate a gate that doesn't cover it.
- WORDS-ONLY MAGNITUDE SMUGGLING: at tier 1 the LLM (i) classifies the case into a taxonomy cell that selects the donor surface and (ii) implicitly scopes the mechanism shift for a novel change. If two plausible taxonomy cells carry opposite-sign surfaces, the LLM's words-only classification is choosing the sign — the magnitude-blindness wall (disproven result #4) re-entering as retrieval. The claim 'no words-only prediction path anywhere in the ladder' is false as written.
- TIER-1 'AGGREGATES ONLY' IS AN ENGINEERING RELABEL: the dbt package computing segment_moments (mediator means, mediator–outcome correlations, segment tables) must run over per-user event-level rows that exist, are joined, and have mediators DEFINED — i.e., the same feature-store modeling work as tier 3, with aggregation applied at the last step. It genuinely reduces the privacy/DPA ask (rows never leave) but barely reduces the onboarding-engineering ask, which is the friction the proposal claims to solve. Also, second moments do not pin nonlinear response surfaces, and segment-level mediator–outcome correlations invite ecological/Simpson inversion unless the spec forces within-segment individual-level computation.
- THE BACKTEST CERTIFICATE IS STATISTICALLY THIN AND LEAKY: typical exhaust is tens of experiments, mostly nulls (Azevedo's fat tails ⇒ most effects ≈ 0, direction undefined/chance by construction), survivorship-biased, with degraded descriptions. A per-customer calibration curve from ~15 resolvable cases has confidence intervals spanning chance-to-excellent — the trust artifact can't bear the weight the commercial story puts on it. And time-masking the data does not mask the translator: experiment descriptions ('we rolled it back', famous public tests in the LLM's training data) leak outcomes into the mechanism spec.
- DONOR CHICKEN-AND-EGG: tier 1 works only after a library of tier-3 donor surfaces exists per taxonomy cell — i.e., it removes onboarding friction for customer N by imposing full tier-3 friction on customers 1..k, and today k ≈ 1 design partner + Senu, whose ₹40 Tamil Nadu game-template respondents have a severe external-validity gap to a paying customer's users. Transport falsification against the customer's own exhaust is exactly weakest for the thin-exhaust customers tier 1 targets — the mitigation is circular.
- ASYMMETRIC BATTERY INFORMATIVENESS: AMBDOM cells are synthetic with (likely near-Gaussian) known generative structure, so the Gaussian-copula ensemble may contain the truth by construction — a PASS on 9/12 synthetic flips is weak evidence for real-world tier 1 (Cookie Cats is the lone real case, n=1, and B2 already showed real datasets are one-sign). Only a FAIL is decision-grade; the proposal sells the pass as promotion-grade.

Evidence collisions: Clean on #1 (persona role-play: no LLM ever emits an outcome), #3 (council confined to the validated abstain-siren role), #5 (production optimism: default-abstain + per-customer backtest is a real structural defense, though weakened by the thin-certificate flaw), and #6 (segment-level claims only, explicit abstain tail). PARTIAL COLLISION with #2/#4 (words-only chance + magnitude-blindness): the tier-1 path routes sign-determining information through words twice — taxonomy classification selects the donor response surface, and the mechanism-shift scoping for a novel change has no data source at tier 1 — so on exactly the AMBDOM flip regime, a misclassified or mis-scoped case produces a confident-wrong verdict through the LLM, the failure mode the campaign measured at 0/12. Its stated defense ('words become mechanism specs, the data computes') holds at tier 3 where GNCE validated the translator against the customer's own observed mediators, but at tier 1 the 'data' doing the computing is donor data selected by words. It would plausibly have FAILED a tier-1 AMBDOM re-run as currently gated (the gate doesn't ensemble surface-transport or Δmediator error); to its genuine credit, the proposal pre-registers exactly that battery with a kill condition — the collision is testable rather than hidden. It also inherits GNCE's boundary honestly (unobserved mediator proxy → abstain) and the negative-control guard's known real-confounding failure (proximal upgrade correctly deferred to tier 3).

Data-ask honesty: Mixed — roughly one-third true reduction, two-thirds relabel/displacement. TRUE REDUCTION: tier 2's one-CSV audit is a genuinely cheaper ask than a per-user feature store, and warehouse-side compute genuinely reduces the PRIVACY/DPA ask at every tier (rows never leave — this is real and sellable). RELABEL: tier 1's segment_moments still requires the customer to stand up per-user event data with mediators defined so the dbt package can aggregate it — the feature-engineering cost (the actual onboarding friction) is nearly unchanged; the ask shrank in bytes exported, not in work. DISPLACEMENT: the magnitude information the g-formula needs is displaced onto (a) donor tier-3 customers and Senu — a chicken-and-egg plus an external-validity gap, so the ask moved from this customer to other customers and to ₹40 respondents — and (b) abstention: where moments don't pin the trade-off the product output is 'grant rows or run the test', which is honest but means tier 1 may deliver confident verdicts only on cases the PM already gets right, i.e., the reduction is paid for in coverage. Honest headline: the ladder reduces WHO must trust you with rows, much less WHAT must exist and be modeled.

**Strongest version (repair):** Keep the four-layer skeleton but repair five things before spending the battery. (1) Split the two businesses honestly: sell the tier-2 audit + sealed backtest as a data-acquisition and trust product for exhaust-RICH customers (with a preregistered minimum-exhaust bar, e.g. ≥30 resolvable non-null experiments, below which no certificate is issued; platform-export required, never curated decks; translator sees a description scrubbed of outcome tells), and admit the prediction buyer enters via a tier-3 pilot or a Senu-quoted micro-study — not via tier 1. (2) Rebuild the gate as a FULL-PRODUCT ensemble: sign must be stable jointly across (copula dependence) × (donor-surface posterior: multiple donors per taxonomy cell + surface hyperparameters) × (Δmediator plausible range) — confident direction only when all axes agree; this is what makes 'zero confident-wrong' a property of the design rather than a hope. (3) Ban words from picking the sign: taxonomy classification becomes another ensemble axis — if any plausible cell/donor flips the sign, automatic abstain — so the LLM can misclassify without ever producing a confident-wrong verdict; add an adversarial wrong-donor arm to the battery to verify this. (4) Make Senu the tier-1 magnitude supplier with a concrete SKU: the 'data quote' at tier 0 prices a matched-panel micro-study that MEASURES the change→mediator shift (₹40/respondent), so Δmediator comes from data, never from prose — this finally gives Senu a load-bearing role consistent with the factory thesis. (5) Re-scope the battery's meaning: keep the ≥9/12 flip-cells / zero-confident-wrong kill condition, but pre-register that a synthetic PASS is necessary-not-sufficient (Gaussian-copula ensemble can contain synthetic truth by construction); tier-1 promotion additionally requires the real Cookie Cats moments-only recovery plus at least one true cross-dataset transport cell, and market tier 1 as 'privacy-preserving' (rows never leave) rather than 'no feature store needed'. With these repairs the proposal is the strongest ladder on the table: evidence-compliant, buildable on the existing runstore/prereg harness, and — its best trait — it converts the open tier-1 question into a cheap, sealed, pre-registered kill experiment in the house style. Run the amended battery; let it decide whether the ladder has a middle rung or collapses to audit-wedge + tier-3 upsell, and be at peace with either answer.

### Judge 3 — top pick: The Backtest Ladder
Through the venture/product-pragmatics lens, the decisive facts are in the founder readout itself: traction is the weak spot (3.5/10), the 4-week priority is a paid pilot or LOI, and the moat is honest calibration plus a data factory that does not yet exist. The Backtest Ladder is the only proposal whose first shippable layer is an invoiceable artifact on data a customer exports in an afternoon (the winner's-curse portfolio audit), whose contract simultaneously solves the cold-start data problem every other proposal hand-waves (the audit IS the data grant), and whose sealed per-customer backtest is the CONTRADICTS demo the YC plan explicitly asks for. Its repaired form is also the most honest about market structure — splitting exhaust-rich audit buyers from thin-exhaust prediction buyers instead of pretending one wedge serves both. The other proposals are best absorbed as components: the Abstention Ledger (proposal 3) is the trust stamp that should gate the Backtest Ladder's verdicts; the Query Compiler's zero-egress warehouse-native mode is the right deployment SKU for the tier-3 upsell; the Transport Ladder's personas-as-P*(z) reframe and computed-interval abstention should be adopted wherever transport is attempted; and the B-PFN's 2-day arithmetic-sufficiency calculator test should run immediately, with the PFN itself gated behind Senu proving learnable signal. B-PFN has the highest research ceiling and the deepest eventual moat, but it spends assets (Senu triples, cross-customer exhaust) before acquiring them — a months-scale bet a pre-traction company cannot lead with. The Backtest Ladder gets to revenue and labeled data fastest while keeping every disproven path (persona role-play, words-only verdicts, calibrated point estimates) on an explicit kill-list, and it converts the open tier-1 middle-rung question into a cheap pre-registered kill experiment rather than a funding assumption. That is what a design partner pays for now, and what makes the rest of the portfolio fundable later.
