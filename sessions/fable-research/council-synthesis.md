# Council Final Synthesis — The Data-Efficiency Frontier for Trustworthy Pre-Test Prediction

*Council chair synthesis · six lenses, six red-teams, three judges · grounded in the sealed campaign evidence (persona role-play 0.29; words-only ≤ chance on flips at any model strength; grounded g-formula 12/12 on magnitude flips; NC-guard LEAD with one real failure; Fragile-Families ceiling). Every claim below either traces to a measured result or is flagged as a bet with a pre-registered kill.*

---

## 0. The one-paragraph verdict

The council did not find a method that dissolves the data dependency, and it stopped looking for one — the evidence says the magnitude information that decides counter-intuitive cases is *physically absent* from words, from persona aggregates, and (for a genuinely novel change) from any tier of the customer's historical data. What the council found instead is that the dependency can be **restructured**: the response-surface and prior knowledge move to Sidira's side (library + factory), the customer's ask drops to what they can export in an afternoon (experiment exhaust, then standardized aggregate tables), the *missing* magnitude for a novel change becomes a **purchasable measurement** (a priced Senu micro-study) instead of an LLM hallucination, and every rung's output is gated by a computed, audited abstention boundary. The honest frontier is: **tier 0/1 buys triage and a priced data quote; tier 2 buys customer-calibrated trust and reversal of past decisions; verdict-grade calls on the counter-intuitive ROI slice are available below tier 3 only where a measured Δmediator or a validated donor surface exists.** Three independent judges and six independent red-teams converged on exactly this frontier from different directions. That convergence is the answer.

## 1. THE UNIFIED ARCHITECTURE

One system, four roles, one ladder. The skeleton is the Backtest Ladder (systems-pragmatist), the verdict-admission policy is MCMS's gate stack, the trust surface is the Abstention Ledger, the deployment mode is the zero-egress warehouse app, and the magnitude supply chain is Senu-as-measurement-SKU. Personas survive in exactly one mathematical role.

### 1.1 The four roles

**WHO PREDICTS — the grounded computation, never a language model.**
The only verdict-emitting engine at any tier is the validated causal stack: direct causal estimate where the lever varied historically; g-formula mediation simulation where the mechanism is observed (the measured 1.00-on-flips regime); transported donor response surfaces run over a moment-matched synthetic population where the customer lacks per-user data. Three hard rules resolve the proposals' conflicts:

1. **Magnitudes must be data-derived or purchased — never LLM-declared** (query-compiler red team's rule, adopted engine-wide). The change→mediator shift for a novel change comes from: observed natural variation, the customer's own exhaust, a validated donor surface, or a priced Senu matched-panel micro-study (~₹40/respondent) that *measures* it. If none exists, the mandatory output is ABSTAIN. This is the single rule that prevents magnitude-blindness re-entering through any back door (mechanism-spec shifts, taxonomy retrieval, PFN priors — all three red-teams found the same smuggling route independently).
2. **Words never pick the sign.** The LLM's taxonomy classification and donor-surface selection are treated as an *uncertainty axis*, not a decision: if any plausible taxonomy cell or donor surface flips the sign, automatic abstain.
3. **Verdicts require full-product sign stability**: the sign must survive a joint ensemble over (copula/dependence structure of the synthetic population) × (donor-surface posterior, multiple donors per cell) × (Δmediator plausible range) × (taxonomy-cell assignment). Zero-confident-wrong becomes a design property, not a hope. This supersedes the Transport Ladder's intersection-of-bounds, which the council **rejected** (see 1.3).

**WHO CALIBRATES — the customer's own exhaust, plus a zero-centered library prior.**
Entry artifact is tier 2: the customer's past A/B log (one CSV per experiment: arms, dates, primary + guardrail lifts, per-segment reads where logged). It does four jobs: (a) the **winner's-curse / empirical-Bayes portfolio audit** (Lee–Shen, Azevedo, Coey–Cunningham) — the day-one commercial wedge, pure computation, zero prediction risk, and contractually the *data grant*; (b) the **sealed per-customer backtest** — time-masked, outcome-scrubbed descriptions → mechanism specs → predictions → revealed truth — the trust certificate earned before any forward call; (c) the customer-specific effect-size prior (fat-tailed, near-zero-centered per the Bing corpus — the structural kill of always-predicts-lift); (d) causal moment conditions pinning response slopes for levers within the exhaust's span. Cross-customer/Senu knowledge enters as a **hierarchical random-effects library prior with reported between-source heterogeneity τ², with all of Senu treated as ONE environment** — and it is quarantined: **a flat-prior refit must preserve the sign of any CALLED verdict** (MCMS's gate, judged the best single statistical fix in the packet). If the prior is the verdict, the verdict is an abstain.

**WHO GUARDS — the Abstention Ledger (conformal selective gate + e-process audit).**
Every verdict passes through: the negative-control watchdog (proximal/double-NC upgrade is the named research route for the one real failure); then a **Learn-then-Test / conformal-risk-control abstain gate** calibrated on watchdog-surviving labels (customer exhaust + Senu triples + public RCTs), Mondrian per failure-mode bucket. The word "certificate" is gated on PAC-style coverage with pre-registered n-floors (≈100 marginal / 40–60 per bucket of *trustworthy* labels); below the floor the identical gate ships as "triage — no guarantee," with the n-to-certificate countdown displayed as the onboarding hook. Production identifiability of the certified region is secured by the **randomized-audit clause**: the customer runs a random ε≈10–15% of certified skips as cheap directional holdouts; pricing is on the (1−ε) saved fraction; no audit clause → no certificate, only triage. Trigger polarity is preserved from the campaign: the 3-model heterogeneous-bias panel survives **only** as an abstain siren (disagreement raises caution; agreement never certifies — measured 0/12 when frontier models agree on flips).

**WHO CONVERSES — the LLM, translator and narrator, never the verdict.**
Unchanged from the surviving architecture: English/Figma → mechanism spec (mediator, sibling/cannibalization metric, negative control, confounders, segments, estimand); computed records → the "talk to your users / why" narration, with the one routing rule (explanation → agent answers grounded; new counterfactual → back to the g-formula). Per-person both-arm views stay labeled **calibrated fiction** — reliable in aggregate, fiction per individual (Salganik ceiling honored at every tier).

**Deployment mode (orthogonal to the ladder):** the full row-level estimator — logistic models, per-row overlap check, NC guard — ships as a **zero-egress warehouse-native app** (Snowflake Native App / BigQuery routine); only verdicts, stamps, and aggregate tables leave. This captures the entire privacy/DPA win with none of the sufficiency loss. The council explicitly notes this reduces the data-*sharing* bar, not the data-*existence* bar, and markets it as "privacy-preserving," never "no feature store needed."

**Personas' one surviving role:** the persona pack is the covariate/effect-modifier distribution P\*(z) — the reweighting target for transported surfaces and the composition of the synthetic population. Numbers flow *into* persona-weighted aggregates; persona prompts never flow into imagined numbers. This is the cleanest survival of the 0.29 result and every lens kept it.

### 1.2 The ladder, rung by rung (what runs where)

- **Tier 0 (persona aggregates only).** No verdicts, ever — measured chance-or-worse, and the council refuses to fake it. Ships in minutes: mechanism-spec structuring of the backlog; failure-mode taxonomy classification; breadth-of-risk checklist (guardrail metrics the PM forgot); library base rates *labeled prior-not-prediction*; the abstain siren; and the **data quote** — the exact tables (tier 1), exhaust (tier 2), or priced Senu micro-study that would unlock each hypothesis, ranked by expected value of information. Tier 0's product is time-to-first-artifact and a priced path up the ladder.
- **Tier 1 (aggregate statistics — ONE standardized cross-tab schema: segment × mediator-bin × outcome, plus segment moments).** Two engines: (a) the **deterministic composition calculator** — Simpson/mix-flip and gross-vs-net arithmetic over the standardized tables, wired to the translator (~50 lines; if experiment E1 confirms sufficiency, this is the tier-1 wedge, shipped in weeks); (b) **transported g-formula over a moment-matched synthetic population**, gated by the full-product ensemble, plus **prior-free sign SETS** (Manski-style) where identification fails. Honest expectation, stated in advance: verdict coverage at tier 1 is small and concentrated on composition-driven flips; mediator-driven flips abstain unless a Δmediator measurement is purchased. Tier 1 is defined by ONE fixed export schema so the ask is a single cheap SQL, not a bespoke negotiation per hypothesis.
- **Tier 2 (A/B exhaust — the recommended entry rung).** Everything above, plus the audit wedge, the sealed backtest certificate, customer-calibrated conformal thresholds, and in-span response slopes. Minimum-exhaust bar pre-registered (≈30 resolvable non-null experiments for a certificate; below it, triage + countdown). Platform export required, never a curated deck (survivorship); translator sees outcome-scrubbed descriptions (leakage).
- **Tier 3 (per-user feature store — the premium rung, not the entry toll).** The proven ceiling: per-customer g-formula with full heterogeneity/CATE, proximal double-NC for naturally-confounded exhaust, surrogate-index estimation *only here* (where long-run linkage data exists to validate Prentice surrogacy). Tier-3 customers additionally serve two system roles: **donor** (response surfaces, fitted-only, pooled with opt-in — never rows) and **instrument**: degrade their data to tier-1/2 views and *measure* the accuracy/abstain delta per rung — publishing the data-efficiency frontier as a number instead of an argument (MCMS's best idea; all judges kept it).

### 1.3 What the council REJECTED, and why

| Rejected | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier-0/1 confident verdicts of any kind** | all proposals' original forms | Every red-team independently proved the flip-deciding magnitude is not in cheap-tier data; a Bayesian posterior in non-identified directions *is* the prior — finding #5 re-armed. |
| **Intersection-of-bounds across source environments** | Transport Ladder | Valid only under source independence; a Senu-dominated library shares one pool/incentive/horizon — the council-of-priors 0/12 failure wearing data. Replaced by random-effects pooling with reported τ², Senu = one environment. |
| **Cross-product transport as an identification claim** | Transport Ladder | Bareinboim–Pearl requires shared structural equations; mechanism-tag matching is LLM verbal analogy — reference-class analogy measured NULL. Transport survives only as an ensemble-gated *donor* mechanism, falsified per-customer against exhaust. |
| **The antisymmetric head as a "provable kill" of optimism** | B-PFN | Blocks only marginal bias; content-conditional bias ("new-feature-ish arm ⇒ up") passes straight through. Demoted to marginal-bias removal + mandatory conditional-bias audit. |
| **Moment-tables as sufficient statistics for the validated estimator** | Query Compiler | False for logistic models and per-row overlap checks (the iter0 blindness). Replaced by the in-warehouse full estimator; moment SQL survives only as a labeled lower-trust fallback. |
| **The surrogate bridge at cheap tiers** | Transport Ladder, MCMS | No reachable library source has genuinely long-run outcomes; Prentice violations flip signs. Deferred to tier 3 with per-customer validation. |

## 2. THE DATA-EFFICIENCY FRONTIER

Coverage numbers are *targets to be measured by E3/E4 and the tier-3-instrument program*, not claims; the council explicitly repudiates the invented coverage percentages in the original proposals.

| Data tier (customer ask) | Honestly deliverable | Verdict type | Trust level | Expected abstain (counter-intuitive slice) | Delivering method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 0** — persona pack / aggregates already on hand (minutes) | Backlog structuring; taxonomy classification; guardrail-breadth checklist; library base rates *labeled prior*; flip-siren; **priced data quote / EVOI roadmap** | None (triage ordering only) | Unverified, labeled as such | ~100% abstain on verdicts (by design) | LLM translator + taxonomy + 3-model disagreement siren + EVOI ranker; personas as P\*(z) only |
| **Tier 1** — one standardized cross-tab export (an afternoon of SQL, zero egress; *presupposes the events exist in-warehouse*) | Composition-driven flip calls (mix-flip, gross-vs-net, trade-down) where arithmetic over the tables decides; prior-free sign sets; per-segment direction breadth | Direction, ensemble-gated; sign-sets otherwise | Moderate; every call traces to the customer's own tables or abstains | High — mediator-driven flips abstain unless Δmediator purchased; honest rate is E3's headline number | Deterministic composition calculator (if E1 passes) + transported g-formula under the full-product ensemble + Manski sign-sets |
| **Tier 1.5** — tier 1 + **purchased Senu Δmediator micro-study** (~₹40/respondent, priced per quote) | Mediator-driven flip calls where the measured shift + donor/own response surface pins the net | Direction + CONTRADICTS, gated | Moderate-high; the flip-deciding number is *measured*, external validity flagged and tested | Falls case-by-case as measurements are bought | g-formula with measured Δmediator; NC guard; ensemble gate |
| **Tier 2** — past A/B log CSV (a day; needs ≈30 resolvable non-null experiments for a certificate) | **Winner's-curse portfolio audit** (past launch reversals — day-one ROI); sealed backtest certificate; customer-calibrated abstain thresholds; in-span forward verdicts; which-5-tests-to-run-next | Direction + CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS on in-span levers; certified skip-calls above the n-floor **with audit clause** | High and *quantified per customer* (calibration curve + live e-process) | Moderate on in-span; still high on out-of-span novel flips | EB shrinkage/Lee–Shen audit; sealed backtest; conformal gate + e-process; exhaust as causal moments |
| **Tier 3** — per-user feature store (zero-egress in-warehouse app; the real cost is the modeling work, which this ladder does not remove) | The proven regime: 1.00 on magnitude flips, Cookie-Cats-class negatives recovered; full heterogeneity + segment counterfactuals; proximal double-NC; surrogate index; talk-to-users on computed records | Direction + full CONFIRMS/CONTRADICTS/CAN'T-VALIDATE, flip-slice coverage | Highest; watchdog-gated, per-person views labeled calibrated fiction | Lowest, never zero (Salganik ceiling = the abstain tail, priced not denied) | Per-customer g-formula + watchdog + Ledger; customer doubles as donor + frontier instrument |

**The frontier in one sentence:** validity is constant down the ladder (the gate guarantees it everywhere); *coverage of the sellable counter-intuitive slice* is what each rung buys — and below tier 3 it is bought either with the customer's own exhaust span or with a purchased measurement, never with a prior.

## 3. THE RESEARCH AGENDA

All sealed, pre-registered, fingerprinted before scoring, in the existing runstore/LEDGER discipline. Ordered by cost and by how much each result reshapes what follows. E1–E4 need zero customer data and zero spend.

**E1 — Arithmetic-sufficiency test (2 days, ₹0). RUN FIRST.**
Translator → mechanism spec → deterministic Simpson/composition calculator on tier-1 standardized tables; score on composition-driven AMBDOM flip cells + aggregate-collapsed Cookie Cats; baselines = frontier LLM *given the same tables* (never the words-only 0.00) and chance. **Kill/branch:** calculator wins → ship it as the tier-1 wedge; any learned model must later beat it. Calculator fails → the aggregate information isn't there; tier-1 verdict ambitions die cheaply and the ladder's middle rung is tier 1.5/2.

**E2 — Same-tag, opposite-sign library audit (this week, ₹0).**
Over every case on disk (AMBDOM/GNCE cells, Hillstrom, Cookie Cats, portfolio cases), tag mechanisms with the existing translator; measure how often identical tags carry opposite-signed true effects. **Kill:** if sign-ambiguity within tags is high (pre-register threshold, e.g. >30%), mechanism-tag retrieval is banned from ever selecting a sign — taxonomy stays an ensemble axis forever. (Cheapest possible test of whether matching re-imports magnitude-blindness.)

**E3 — Moment-Degradation Battery, repaired (2 weeks, ₹0).**
Per dataset (12 AMBDOM flip cells, GNCE, real Cookie Cats, Hillstrom, the 10 one-sign B2 sets): degrade to the exact tier-1 export spec (schema authored blind, fingerprinted first); rebuild population via IPF + copula ensemble; transport surfaces cross-dataset where possible; run the unchanged g-formula under the **full-product ensemble** (dependence × donor posterior × Δmediator range × taxonomy cell), including an adversarial wrong-donor arm and a Tier-1-realistic arm where the shift is *not* leaked from the source rows. Pre-registered: ≥9/12 flip cells correct-and-confident with **zero confident-wrong**; Cookie Cats negative recovered from moments alone; zero confident-wrong across the one-sign sets; **the tier-1 abstain rate is a headline number**. **Kill:** any confident-wrong on flips, or <6/12 → tier 1 has no verdict rung; ladder = audit wedge + tier 1.5 (purchased measurement) + tier-3 upsell. Pre-registered meta-rule: a synthetic pass is necessary-not-sufficient; promotion needs the real-data legs.

**E4 — Abstention Ledger Part A, repaired (2 weeks, ₹0).**
Pool ~50–70 labeled cases; predictor stack = grounded sim where data exists + trust features; α=0.10; leave-one-bucket-out Mondrian; thresholds sealed before scoring; **real-data held-out coverage required** (Hillstrom, Cookie Cats), minimum non-abstained count per bucket for any pass; falsification arm: the gate wrapped around the words-only predictor must drive flip certification to ~0. **Kill:** coverage violated, grounded-predictor abstention ≈100%, or the falsification arm certifies the chance predictor (gate broken). PPI++ leg (synthetic 100-hypothesis backlog, k=10 active labels, ≥30% CI shrinkage) rides along but is decorative until tier 3.

**E5 — Proximal / double-negative-control on the benchmark that broke the guard (2–3 weeks).**
Does the confounding bridge recover the sign the single NC could only flag? **Kill:** no recovery → naturally-confounded exhaust stays "untrustworthy-empirical → abstain" and the tier-2 label-admission gate tightens accordingly.

**E6 — Senu Δmediator SKU pilot (gated on Senu deployment; first real spend).**
One matched-panel micro-study measuring a change→mediator shift for a case where E3 abstained; predict, seal, compare against a held-out truth source; explicitly test the Senu→target external-validity edge (the transfer is the hypothesis, not the assumption). **Kill:** measured shifts don't transport even directionally → Senu is a calibration-set factory (Mondrian bucket-filler for E4's n-floors), not a magnitude supplier — still valuable, differently priced.

**E7 — B-PFN spike (gated on E1's branch + E3; ≤2 weeks, one GPU).**
Only where the calculator can't go. Train on 5 of 6 taxonomy families + an independently-authored sealed generator, test on the held-out family; baselines = the calculator and same-tables LLM; conditional-bias audit (directional base rate given change-type features on a balanced flip set) with a hard fail threshold; real cases are the only promotable evidence. **Kill:** family-held-out failure, or conditional-bias audit failure → the amortized-prior bet dies before the corpus spend; the feature-store ask is proven irreducible for ₹0, which is itself the answer.

**Standing measurement (not an experiment — the instrument):** every tier-3 customer's data is degraded to tier-1/2 views and re-scored, permanently, publishing the per-rung accuracy/abstain delta. This is how the frontier table in §2 becomes a maintained empirical object — and a publishable one.

## 4. DISSENTS

Recorded honestly; none were resolved by fiat — each carries a decision rule.

1. **Top pick split (2–1).** Judges 1 (decision-theoretic) and 3 (venture) picked the **Backtest Ladder** — first deliverable changes real decisions in days, the audit contract is the data grant, Senu gets a load-bearing SKU. Judge 2 (scientific soundness) picked the **Abstention Ledger** — the only proposal whose every claim is a finite-sample guarantee or a labeled non-claim, and the only artifact a hostile DS team cannot shred. The chair's resolution (Ledger = mandatory gate layer *inside* the Backtest Ladder skeleton) is a synthesis both would sign, but the emphasis dispute is real: **is the company an audit-led wedge with a guarantee inside, or a guarantee-led instrument with an audit on top?** This is a go-to-market decision the evidence cannot make.
2. **Does tier 1 exist as a verdict rung at all?** The MCMS and Query-Compiler red-teams argue honest tier-1 collapses to near-total abstain (Manski sets straddle zero on 1–3pp effects; marginal moments can't identify slopes); the Backtest Ladder and B-PFN lenses maintain composition-driven flips are genuinely arithmetic over aggregates. Unresolved by argument; **E1 + E3's abstain-rate headline resolves it empirically.** The council's synthesis deliberately prices both branches.
3. **Is the audit wedge defensible?** Judge 3 flags commoditization (Eppo/Statsig can ship EB shrinkage natively) and target-market inversion (exhaust-rich buyers ≠ prediction buyers); Judge 1 counters that the audit's value is the *data grant + trust sequence*, not the stats. Dissent stands: the wedge's moat is the ladder above it, not the audit itself.
4. **Is the randomized-audit clause commercially survivable?** The Ledger's red team notes it eats the skipped-test ROI being sold; the repair prices around it (charge on the (1−ε) fraction). Judge 2 holds it is non-negotiable for identifiability; Judge 3 quietly doubts customers sign it. If they won't, certificates degrade to triage — the product survives, the *word* doesn't.
5. **B-PFN's worth.** Judge 3 scores it the highest ceiling and deepest eventual moat; Judge 1 scores its verdict path the riskiest on the table (prior-dominated exactly on the sellable slice — finding #5 with a trained prior). Both endorse the same sequencing (calculator first, corpus spend gated), so the dissent is about *hope*, not action.
6. **How much was the data ask actually reduced?** The sharpest red-team finding, endorsed across lenses: most "tier-1" designs reduce data *egress* (real: DPA/privacy is often the binding enterprise friction) while leaving data *existence and modeling* untouched — the events must still exist, joined, with mediators defined. The council adopts the honest phrasing ("privacy-preserving," "who must trust you with rows, not what must exist") over any lens's original framing, and records that a minority view considers even this synthesis's ladder partly a relabel.
7. **Senu's role.** Magnitude supplier (Backtest Ladder), prior/corpus factory (B-PFN, MCMS), or Mondrian-bucket calibration filler (Ledger)? All three roles are claimed; E6 decides which Senu actually is. The external-validity gap (Tamil Nadu panel game-tasks → a customer's users) is load-bearing in every role and validated in none — the council's single largest shared exposure.

## 5. WHAT ANTHROPIC WOULD DO DIFFERENTLY

1. **Ship the gate before the oracle.** The Abstention Ledger (~600 lines on existing assets) wraps the *current* engine immediately — it would have converted the production blind-test failures into measured abstentions instead of confident wrongs. The safety layer is not downstream of the capability; it is the first capability.
2. **Treat the product as a measurement instrument for its own frontier.** The tier-3-customer degradation program makes "what does each data rung buy" a continuously published number. Anthropic would run the evals harness as the product's core loop, not its pre-launch phase — and publish the frontier table as the research paper.
3. **Never train where the information question is unsettled.** Run E1's ₹0 calculator test and E2's tag audit before any corpus, any PFN, any RL. If a 50-line deterministic program ties the proposed model, the model is unfunded. (The campaign already lived this discipline; Anthropic would institutionalize it as a gate on every training dollar.)
4. **Buy missing numbers; never sample them from a prior.** The single most consequential architectural rule in this synthesis: flip-deciding magnitudes are data-derived or purchased (Senu SKU) or the output is abstain. A prior — trained, transported, or prompted — is never a verdict.
5. **Make honesty machine-checkable, not cultural.** Prior-quarantine (flat-prior sign-preservation), the falsification arm (a gate that certifies a chance predictor is broken), the randomized-audit clause, conditional-bias audits on balanced flip sets, git-immutable sealed records — every honesty claim gets an automated adversarial test. Trust that can't be attacked can't be sold.
6. **Design the abstention as the product surface.** "Can't call this — here is the exact table/measurement/experiment that would change that, and its price" is a better answer than a hedged arrow. The EVOI data-quote is the conversion funnel; abstention with a priced next step is retention, not failure.
7. **Standardize one aggregate schema and freeze it.** A single fixed segment × mediator-bin × outcome export spec, versioned like an API — otherwise "tier 1" is the feature-store ask in installments and every onboarding is a bespoke negotiation.
8. **Keep the LLM where it is superhuman and nowhere else.** Translation (English/Figma → mechanism spec), narration (computed records → interrogable "why"), hypothesis generation, and adversarial spec review. Model upgrades improve the *interface* (Opus 0.68 vs llama 0.42 on translation), never the verdict — this division held under a frontier-model swap and Anthropic would bet it holds under the next one.
9. **Price the ceiling instead of denying it.** The Salganik limit, the calibrated-fiction label on per-person counterfactuals, and the abstain tail are stated in the contract, the UI, and the paper. The coverage-at-guaranteed-error curve *is* the honest quantification of the founder's 80/20 bet — measure it, publish it, and let it be the moat, because every competitor's crystal ball fails exactly the diligence this system is built to survive.
10. **Let experiments kill things in writing.** Every rung of this architecture carries a pre-registered kill criterion, and the org commits — in advance, in git — to being at peace with either answer. The council's own process (proposal → red-team → repair → judges → synthesis) converged only because each layer was allowed to kill the one before it. Keep that machine running; it is the actual research method this paper should describe.
