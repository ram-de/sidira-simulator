## The agreed answer to the original question

*"Grounded sim + watchdog needs data customers won't hand over on day one; persona-seeded LLM agents are disproven; how do we achieve accuracy?"*

**Both councils, independently and after debate:** the data dependency cannot be dissolved — the magnitudes that decide counter-intuitive cases are physically absent from personas, prose, and priors — but it can be **restructured** into a ladder where every rung is honest:

> **Sidira is not a lower-data oracle. It is a selective decision system: it calls only the estimands whose missing quantities have been measured, imported from measured evidence under a transport gate, or computed from sufficient aggregate tables; otherwise it abstains and names the cheapest measurement that would change that fact.** *(codex wording, adopted verbatim.)*

> **The policy target is constant — bounded directional error on the called set — but the evidentiary basis, estimand, and coverage narrow sharply as customer data decreases.** *(Replaces Fable's original "validity is constant down the ladder, coverage varies" — retired as an overclaim.)*

## The proven-engines statement (joint, binding on the roadmap)

> The only currently proven verdict engines are: **(1) deterministic arithmetic over sufficient aggregate tables** (E1: 24/24 incl. 12/12 misleading-narrative flips; real Cookie Cats signs + magnitudes recovered from tier-1 moments alone), **(2) direct randomized/historical A/B estimates** where an equivalent lever varied, and **(3) grounded g-formula/mediation simulation** where the customer's data and identification checks support it (AMBDOM 12/12). Every other path — donor surfaces, Senu micro-measurements, learned tabular response models, amortized causal models/PFNs, RL, mechanism retrieval — is an **abstention test** until it beats the relevant proven engine under seal, **on decision economics** (bounded false-ship/false-kill at useful coverage, against PM-hypothesis, always-flat, customer base-rate, EB-shrinkage, calculator, and LLM-with-same-tables baselines), not just accuracy.

## What the two experiments settled

**E1 (PASS)** — the tier-1 verdict rung *exists*, scoped exactly: verdicts are **arithmetic verdicts over tables that contain the sufficient observed quantities** (arm/pilot aggregates, shift shares, bin rates). Composition-driven flips are 100% callable from the frozen schema; the words-only 0.00 → with-tables 1.00 jump (both calculator and Opus) settles that magnitude-blindness is **information absence, not reasoning inability**. The calculator stays the shipping path on reliability (Opus made one silent arithmetic slip on an easy cell). What E1 does **not** prove: tier-1 inference of unobserved treatment shifts from prose (that stays abstain/data-quote), or how much of a real backlog is composition-driven (**prevalence — the next experiment**).

**E2 (KILL)** — mechanism-family tags carry almost no sign information: 13/15 real in-group cases (86.7%, Wilson 95% [62.1%, 96.3%], threshold 30%) sit in sign-ambiguous families; the finer grain evaporates into singletons. **Mechanism tags never pick signs — not as retrieval, not as "soft" tier-0 family priors.** Tags survive as uncertainty axes, retrieval organizers, and coverage bookkeeping only. Tier-0 priors must be zero-centered effect-size priors, never directional mechanism lore.
