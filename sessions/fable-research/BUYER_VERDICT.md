## The verdict on the literal question

**No. P(Amazon-class account pays within 24 months) = 5%** (Fable ~4%, codex 5–8%, joint planning number 5%). The kill is overdetermined:

1. **They authored the fix.** The winner's-curse/shrinkage literature the tier-2 wedge implements came out of Amazon-class experimentation orgs. Selling it there is selling the textbook back to its authors.
2. **The measured numbers don't clear their bar.** E3-P: 2.3% pre-launch prevalence, 13.6% post-pilot coverage, called accuracy tying PM optimism — and traffic is not scarce at Amazon; the cheapest measurement is the test itself.
3. **Procurement physics.** Build-vs-buy reflex + security review kill the data-access rung (tier 3) that carries the only proven predictive edge.

The Amazon-class objection is not "this is wrong." It is worse commercially: **"this is correct, narrow, and buildable."** (codex R1 — adopted as the joint one-liner.)

Codex standing amendment (adopted): central-experimentation-science orgs are bad *first-year* targets, not permanent non-buyers — a later sale is conceivable after sealed certificates + zero-egress deployment exist, and the residual 5% is an acqui-hire/research-consult/test-starved-BU tail. Amazon's correct role in year one: **alumni pipeline** — the ex-Weblab scientist at a mid-market company is the champion who knows the math is real and knows their employer can't staff it.

## The two load-bearing unknowns

1. **DEMAND (fatal-unless-disproven; Fable red team, adopted by codex as its "most important correction").** Winner's curse presents internally as *success* — metrics up, launch shipped, champion promoted. The audit indicts the buyer's past decisions and can embarrass its own sponsor. Repair: dollar-denominated, forward-looking framing ("this shipped win will miss its annualized projection by $X; here's the cheap holdout that proves it") + champion archetypes with someone else to blame. There is **zero conversion evidence either way**; this is now a registered hypothesis with receipt metrics, not an assumption.
2. **CUSTOMER-SIDE COVERAGE.** E3-P's 13.6% is a public-artifact floor; the ownership argument says owned pilot exports lift it, but that is unmeasured. Joint threshold (scoped to the *recurring subscription*, not the audit): **≥40% = subscription thesis live; 30–40% = probation (zero confident-wrong + strong receipts required); <30% = kill the recurring gate, sell audit/triage only.** One confident-wrong sign in the wild separately kills the "never confidently ship the wrong sign" claim until the admission rule is repaired and revalidated.

## The irreproducible-asset answer (joint, unflinching)

**Today, in code or method: nothing.** The arithmetic is ~50 lines; the shrinkage is published; the abstention ledger is a sprint. What cannot be cloned in a sprint: the **sealed negative-evidence corpus** (diligence value — a reason to trust us and distrust "AI-agents-simulate-your-users" competitors), and *prospectively* the **per-customer sealed backtest certificates + cross-customer measured-triples library** — the only compounding asset, and it must be earned customer by customer. 6. **Guard/factory kills:** E5 fails (watchdog unusable on confounded exhaust) / E6 fails (triples don't transfer — the compounding asset evaporates).
