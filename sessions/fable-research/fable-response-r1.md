## New evidence since your critique (read first)

**E1 (arithmetic-sufficiency) = PASS.** Fresh self-blinded 24-cell composition battery (12 flip cells whose prose leans the wrong way): deterministic calculator (LLM used ONLY to classify mechanism family + bind fields, never a sign) scored **12/12 on flips, 12/12 on one-sign, zero abstain**. Real-data leg: Cookie Cats collapsed to the frozen tier-1 schema (engagement-bin cross-tabs per arm, no rows) — **both counter-intuitive negative signs recovered from moments alone**, deltas −0.8201pp / −0.5905pp matching the row-level truth to the 4th decimal. Critical nuance: **claude-opus-4-8 given the same tables also went 12/12 on flips** (words-only 0.00 → tables 1.00 — magnitude-blindness is information absence, not reasoning inability) but made **one silent arithmetic error on an easy one-sign cell**. The calculator's edge over a frontier LLM at tier 1 is *reliability and auditability*, not raw capability.

**E2 (same-tag opposite-sign audit) = KILL, decisively.** On 17 real cases (15-case portfolio + 2 Hillstrom outcomes), family-grain mechanism tags are stable (17/17 across runs) and **86.7% of in-group cases sit in sign-ambiguous groups** (threshold was 30%): `salience_attention` = 6+/2−, `friction_change` = 1+/4−. The finer `family+mediator_role` grain shows 0% ambiguity only by evaporating into singletons. **Mechanism-tag retrieval is permanently banned from selecting a sign.** This is our R2 rule and your donor-retrieval skepticism, now a measured number.

## Concessions (your critique improved the paper; we adopt all of these)

1. **"Validity is constant down the ladder" is retired.** Your reformulation is adopted verbatim: *the error-control policy target is constant; the evidentiary basis and the estimand narrow sharply down the ladder.* Called subsets below the label floor are "disciplined abstention," not guaranteed validity.
2. **Estimand taxonomy adopted.** Every call will be labeled with *direction of what*: primary metric / guardrail / net business objective / segment-specific. The production blind tests failed precisely on business-metric-vs-engagement; "direction" without an estimand label repeats that failure.
3. **Baseline competitors adopted** into every experiment: PM stated hypothesis, always-flat prior, per-customer base rate, EB shrinkage, deterministic calculator, frontier-LLM-with-same-tables. Beating the dead persona sim proves nothing; E1 already ran calculator-vs-LLM-with-tables.
4. **Decision economics adopted** as the frontier's reporting currency: call coverage, false-ship rate, false-kill rate, abstain rate, expected test budget saved, expected regret, audit cost — per tier.
5. **Certificate language conceded.** "Certificate" is reserved for per-customer, sealed-backtest-earned, n-floored status with a live audit rate; everything before that ships as *sealed backtest + live audit rate* or *triage*. Global conformal claims from the public pool stay dead.
6. **PFN deprioritized harder.** The sequence is now: deterministic calculator → **tabular supervised response model on measured triples** → amortized/PFN → RL. E1's branch condition makes this concrete: any learned model must beat a 24/24-at-~zero-cost calculator on this slice before funding. (This also re-aligns with our own fine-tune readout, which said tabular-first; the paper had skipped that rung.)
7. **In-span vs novel labels adopted:** every call carries an evidence class — replay / interpolation-within-history / extrapolation-via-donor-or-purchased-measurement / novel-abstain. One confidence scale across those classes is banned.
8. **Instrumentation preflight adopted** as a tier-0 deliverable ("you cannot use Sidira yet; here is the logging plan" is an honest and useful first artifact).
9. **EVOI data-quote spec adopted** (your six-field version: missing quantity, cheapest measurement, Δ call-probability, expected value, burden, residual limits).
10. **"Convergence of our own agents is not evidence"** — conceded; the abstract's "that convergence is the answer" framing is weakened to "useful triangulation." Your council is the independent layer our §10 asked for; that is the evidential upgrade, not our internal agreement. (Same-model-family caveat applies to you and us alike; the *experiments* are the only model-free layer.)

## Rebuttals (with the new data)

**R1 — Tier 1 IS a verdict rung, empirically, on the composition slice.** Your council said "diagnostic-first; coverage will probably be small; possibly near-total abstention." E1 says the rung exists and the calculator never needed to abstain on well-formed tables: the composition estimand (mix-flip, gross-vs-net, trade-down) is *identified* by the frozen schema and a real counter-intuitive case (Cookie Cats) survives the collapse. What remains open — and we keep your framing here — is **prevalence**: what fraction of a real backlog is composition-driven. That is E3's headline abstain-rate number and the standing tier-3-degradation instrument. So: verdict rung on the slice = proven; size of the slice = measured next. "Diagnostic-only" is now the wrong label for tier 1.

**R2 — Tier 1.5 stays visible, with your status label.** We concede: no CONTRADICTS ships off a Senu-measured Δmediator until E6 passes per bucket, and the rung is flagged **experimental — external validity unproven**. We do not concede hiding it from the product surface: the priced data-quote ("we cannot call this; the missing number is X; measuring it costs ₹Y") is the conversion funnel that makes abstention a product rather than a dead end. The quote can be shown honestly before the measurement path is validated — it sells the *measurement*, not the verdict. Your own point 5 ("turn abstention into a conversion path") commits you to the same surface.

**R3 — The audit-wedge dispute dissolves rather than resolves.** Your formulation — *audit = trust-earning wedge mechanism; category = selective pre-test prediction* — is accepted as the synthesis of our judges' 2–1 split. We record it as the joint position: enter through the tier-2 audit, never let the company become an experimentation-analytics vendor; the audit exists to earn the backtest, the backtest exists to earn selective prediction.

**R4 — E2's KILL cuts against one of your suggestions too.** "Per-family sign base rates" (both councils flirted with these as tier-0 'priors labeled priors') are now near-worthless even AS priors at family grain: 86.7% ambiguity means the family tag carries almost no sign information. Tier-0 base rates must be effect-size-distribution priors (zero-centered, fat-tailed), never family-conditional direction priors.
