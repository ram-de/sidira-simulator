== HEADLINE (the clean causal directional bit) ==
by-arm retention_7 direction_accuracy = 1.0 => the simulator predicted the SAME (negative) sign as
the real RCT: it correctly predicted that moving the gate LATER (gate_40) LOWERS 7-day retention.
This is the COUNTER-INTUITIVE winner (naive prior says "later gate = more free play = better"); the
sim did NOT echo the naive prior. ** First time the sim picked a counter-intuitive REAL product-change
winner correctly on external ground truth. **
CAVEAT (pre-registered, decisive): n_cases=1 -> the null-integrity certificate is VOID (a single binary
label cannot be scrambled, so the permutation null has no power to rule out leakage). So this is a
SUGGESTIVE correct bit, NOT a certified result. It cannot, by itself, be trusted as leakage-free.

== THE CERTIFIABLE ENDPOINTS SHOW NO SIGNAL ==
The only endpoints that clear the 8-case null-cert floor are the by-segment (engagement-octile) runs,
and BOTH are at chance: retention_7 rank_rho -0.46 (p=0.89), dir_acc 0.125; retention_1 rank_rho +0.05
(p=0.46), dir_acc 0.25. CRITICAL: by-segment stratifies on sum_gamerounds, a POST-TREATMENT mediator
(codex P1, pre-registered) -> the within-octile contrasts are confounded/DESCRIPTIVE, not causal, so
their per-octile "real" signs are a distorted, poor scoring target. A null here does NOT cleanly mean
the sim is wrong about the causal effect.

== HONEST VERDICT ==
Mixed, and structurally so. Cookie Cats is a SINGLE experiment with NO pre-treatment covariates, so:
  - the only CLEAN causal question (whole-population sign) is n=1 -> uncertifiable (VOID), and there the
    sim got the counter-intuitive PRIMARY-metric sign RIGHT (encouraging, suggestive);
  - the only CERTIFIABLE endpoint (8 octiles) is confounded by a post-treatment stratifier -> shows no
    signal, as a confounded target plausibly would.
=> This is the FIRST in-scope external signal pointing the RIGHT way, but it is NOT a certified positive.
   Cookie Cats cannot deliver a certified in-scope positive on its own (one experiment, no covariates).
   A certified in-scope positive needs MULTIPLE independent real product-change experiments (or one with
   genuine pre-treatment covariates to support clean CATEs). Secondary metric (retention_1) is a weak
   target: the real ret1 effect is near-null/non-significant, and by-arm ret1 got the sign wrong but is
   VOID anyway -> do not over-read.
