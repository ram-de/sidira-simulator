### User goal
Manage my Streamly plan and subscription.

### Roles
* member
* premium

### Flow per role
#### member
1. The member lands on the `/account/plan` screen with headings "Plan / Current plan".
2. The member can click the "Upgrade to Premium" button to transition to another `/account/plan` screen.
3. The member can also navigate to other capabilities by clicking links such as "STREAMLY", "Browse", "Search", "My List", "Kids", their username ("Sam Member"), or sign out.

#### premium
1. The premium user lands on the `/account/plan` screen with headings "Plan / Current plan / Downloads".
2. The premium user can click the "Accept" button to transition to another `/account/plan` screen.
3. The premium user can also navigate to other capabilities by clicking links such as "STREAMLY", "Browse", "Search", "My List", "Kids", their username ("Pat Premium"), or sign out.
