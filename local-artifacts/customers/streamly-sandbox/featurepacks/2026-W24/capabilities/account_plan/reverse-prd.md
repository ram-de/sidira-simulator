### 1. User goal
Manage my Streamly plan and subscription.

### 2. Roles
The following roles were observed:
- member
- premium

### 3. Flow per role
#### Member
1. The member starts on the `/account/plan` screen with headings "Plan / Current plan".
2. The member can click the "Upgrade to Premium" button to transition to another `/account/plan` screen.
3. The member can exit to other capabilities by clicking links such as "STREAMLY", "Browse", "Search", "My List", "Kids", their username, or the "Sign Out" button.

#### Premium
1. The premium user starts on the `/account/plan` screen with headings "Plan / Current plan / Downloads".
2. The premium user can click the "Accept" button to transition to another `/account/plan` screen.
3. The premium user can exit to other capabilities by clicking links such as "STREAMLY", "Browse", "Search", "My List", "Kids", their username, or the "Sign Out" button.
